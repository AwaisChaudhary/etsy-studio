Sweater size calculator

Which size sweater should you knit or crochet? Enter your chest or bust measurement and the fit you like, and get the finished size to choose, the ease you'd get in each pattern size, and an estimate of how much yarn to buy.

Quick answer: measure around the fullest part of your chest or bust, then add the ease for the fit you want and pick the pattern size whose finished chest is closest. The Craft Yarn Council's guide: very close fitting is about 2 to 4 in (5 to 10 cm) less than your body, close fitting is the same, classic fit is 2 to 4 in more, loose fit 4 to 6 in more, and oversized 6 in (15 cm) or more. A 38 in chest in a classic fit wants a sweater of about 40 to 42 in. Yarn: an adult worsted pullover takes roughly 1,100–1,300 yd knitted, more if crocheted.

Copy the "finished measurements" or "actual chest" numbers, not the "to fit" ones.

Lengths and sleeve lengths are our typical figures, so measure yourself or a sweater you like. Cables, texture and crochet use more. Your pattern's yardage always wins: buy that, plus a skein.

How do I measure myself for a sweater?

  • Chest or bust: around the fullest part, under the arms, with the tape level and snug but not tight. Wear what you'd wear under the sweater.
  • Length: from the top of the shoulder, next to the neck, down to where you want the hem. Hip length is typical; cropped is shorter.
  • Sleeve: from the underarm to the wrist with your arm relaxed.

Easier still: lay a sweater that fits the way you like flat, measure across the chest just under the arms and double it. That's the finished chest to look for in a pattern, ease already included.

What is ease in knitting and crochet?

Ease is the difference between your body and the finished garment. A 38 in chest in a 42 in sweater has 4 in of positive ease, so it hangs a little loose. Negative ease, a sweater smaller than you, works only for stretchy fabric like ribbing and fitted tops. Hats and socks are usually made with negative ease too so they stay put; our hat size chart and sock size chart build it in.

Which size should I make if I'm between sizes?

Go by the fit the designer shows. Most patterns say how much ease the sample has ("shown with 4 in of positive ease"): add that to your chest and pick the nearest finished size. If you're between two, the larger one is usually the safer choice for a pullover, and the smaller one for a cardigan you'll wear open or a fitted top. Check the length and sleeves too: those are easy to change while you knit, the chest isn't.

Why does crochet take more yarn than knitting?

Crochet stitches are thicker and denser, so the same area uses more yarn, roughly a quarter more by our estimate. A crocheted sweater can also feel heavier, so choose a lighter yarn or an open stitch for drape. Whichever you make, a gauge swatch tells you whether your size will come out right: compare it with the pattern in the gauge converter.

How many skeins do I need for a sweater?

Divide the yards by the yards on one skein and round up, then buy one more of the same dye lot. Running short on a sweater is painful because the last skein often shows as a stripe if the dye lot changes. Swapping to a different yarn than the pattern uses? Questions crafters ask

What size sweater should I knit?

Add the ease you want to your chest measurement and pick the pattern size whose finished chest is closest. For a classic fit that's about 2 to 4 in more than your chest, so a 38 in chest makes a 40 to 42 in sweater.

How much ease should a sweater have?

The Craft Yarn Council's guide: very close fitting 2 to 4 in less than your body, close fitting 0, classic fit 2 to 4 in more, loose fit 4 to 6 in more, oversized 6 in or more.

How much yarn do I need to knit a sweater?

By our estimate an adult 38 in chest pullover with classic ease and long sleeves takes about 1,100–1,300 yd of worsted, or about 900–1,100 yd of bulky for a 42 in chest. Always check your pattern.

How much yarn do I need to crochet a sweater?

About a quarter more than knitting: roughly 1,300–1,700 yd of worsted for an adult 38 in chest pullover with long sleeves, by our estimate.

What does finished chest mean in a pattern?

The measurement around the sweater itself, ease included. Compare it with your body measurement plus the ease you want, not with your body alone.

Should I size up or down between sweater sizes?

Usually up for a pullover, and down for a fitted top or an open cardigan. Look at how much ease the pattern sample is shown with and match that.
