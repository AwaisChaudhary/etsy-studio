Scarf size chart and yarn calculator

How long and wide a scarf should be for a child, teen, woman or man, plus infinity scarves and cowls. Pick a size and yarn to get the stitches to cast on, the chains to start and the yards to buy.

Quick answer: an adult scarf is usually 60 to 70 in long (150 to 180 cm) and 6 to 8 in wide; men's often run a little longer, up to about 75 in. A child of 6 to 10 needs about 50 to 60 in, and a long scarf that wraps twice 75 to 90 in. An easy rule: make it about as long as the wearer is tall. A 65 × 7 in scarf takes roughly 270–340 yards of worsted knitted, or 340–430 crocheted (our estimates).

Is there a rule for scarf length?

The old rule is to make a scarf about as long as the wearer is tall, or as long as their arm span. It's a starting point, not a law: AllFreeCrochet notes plenty of people prefer shorter. For width, a hand span (thumb to little finger, spread) is a handy guide, and a longer scarf usually looks better a little wider. Choose "As long as the wearer is tall" in the calculator to use it.

How wide should a scarf be?

About 6 to 8 in for most adult scarves, 5 to 7 in for a classic men's scarf, 3 to 5 in for children, and 4 in or less for a skinny scarf. Wraps and stoles are 18 to 22 in wide. Remember that ribbing pulls in, so swatch in the real stitch before you count your cast on.

For a scarf that looks good on both sides and doesn't curl, pick a reversible texture from our knit stitch patterns: seed, moss, mistake rib or garter rib.

How long is an infinity scarf?

An infinity scarf that loops twice is about 56 to 60 in around and 8 to 10 in wide. A cowl that sits close to the neck in one loop is about 24 to 30 in around, and a child's cowl about 18 to 22 in. Knit it flat and sew the ends together (add a twist first for a Möbius look), or work it in the round; see how to knit in the round.

How many stitches do I cast on for a scarf?

Multiply the width by your stitches per inch. At 18 stitches per 4 in (4.5 per inch), a 7 in scarf needs about 32 stitches. In crochet, add the turning chain to the foundation chain. The calculator does this from the typical gauge for your yarn; use your own with the cast on calculator or the foundation chain calculator.

Are scarves safe for toddlers?

Long scarves can catch on things, so many makers skip them for children under about 3 or 4 (AllFreeCrochet raises the same worry) and make a snug cowl or neck warmer instead. For any child's scarf, leave off loose fringe, buttons and pom-poms that could come off.

How much yarn does fringe take?

More than you'd think. Each tassel is folded in half, so every strand is twice the fringe length plus about an inch for the knot. With 4 strands per tassel, a tassel every inch or so, and fringe at both ends, a 7 in wide scarf with 6 in fringe uses about 20 yards. The calculator adds it for you.

Questions crafters ask

How long should a scarf be?

60 to 70 in (150 to 180 cm) for most adults, up to about 75 in for men, 50 to 60 in for children of 6 to 10, and 75 to 90 in for a long scarf that wraps twice. A simple rule is about the wearer's height.

How wide should a scarf be?

About 6 to 8 in for adults, 5 to 7 in for a classic men's scarf, 3 to 5 in for children and 4 in or less for a skinny scarf.

How much yarn do I need for a scarf?

Roughly 270–340 yards of worsted for a 65 × 7 in knitted scarf, or 340–430 crocheted. Bulky yarn takes about 190–240 yards knitted. These are our estimates for plain stitches; ribbing and cables take more.

How long is an infinity scarf?

About 56 to 60 in around and 8 to 10 in wide so it loops twice. A one-loop cowl is about 24 to 30 in around.

How long should a child's scarf be?

About 35 to 40 in for ages 4 to 5 and 50 to 60 in for ages 6 to 10, and 3 to 5 in wide. For toddlers, a cowl is safer.

How many stitches do I cast on for a scarf?

Width times stitches per inch. In worsted at 18 stitches per 4 in, a 7 in scarf is about 32 stitches. Swatch in your stitch pattern first.
