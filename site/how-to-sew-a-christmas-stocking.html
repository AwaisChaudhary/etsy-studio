How to sew a Christmas stocking (free printable template)

A lined Christmas stocking with a cuff is one of the easiest things to sew: two outer pieces, two lining pieces, a cuff and a loop. This beginner guide shows six drawn steps, and the template maker prints a full-size stocking pattern in any size, with the cutting list and how much fabric to buy.

Quick answer: to sew a Christmas stocking, cut 2 outer and 2 lining pieces from a stocking template (fabric folded, right sides together), sew each pair round the sides and foot, and turn the outer right side out. Slip on a folded cuff and a hanging loop, put the outer stocking inside the lining and sew round the top, then turn it through a gap left in the lining and stitch the gap shut. A standard 18 in stocking needs about 1/2 yd of outer fabric and 1/2 yd of lining with 1/2 in seams, by our estimate.

How to sew a Christmas stocking, step by step

What you need

  • Outer fabric: quilting cotton, canvas, velvet, faux fur, wool felt or an old sweater. Quilting cotton is easiest. Add fusible fleece or batting behind the outer pieces for a padded, sturdier stocking.
  • Lining: plain or printed cotton. It hides the seams and stops small gifts catching.
  • Cuff: a contrast cotton, faux fur, velvet or white felt. Felt and fleece don't fray, so they don't need lining.
  • Also: 6 to 8 in of ribbon or a fabric strip for the loop, pins or clips, matching thread, scissors, and a sewing machine or a needle for backstitch by hand.

Sewing tips for a neat stocking

  • Clip the curves: after sewing, snip little notches into the seam allowance round the toe and heel (not through the stitches), so the curves lie smooth when turned.
  • Directional prints: folding the fabric makes the front and back face opposite ways, which is right. With a one-way print, check the pattern runs upright on both.
  • Felt stockings: skip the lining, sew the two pieces wrong sides together 1/4 in from the edge with blanket stitch or a straight stitch, and leave the edges raw.
  • Add a name: stitch it on the cuff before you sew the cuff into a ring. Our how to embroider letters guide prints the name at the right size.

Questions crafters ask

How do you sew a Christmas stocking for beginners?

Cut two outer and two lining pieces from a stocking template, sew each pair round the sides and foot, add a folded cuff and a ribbon loop, put the outer stocking inside the lining and sew round the top. Turn it through a gap in the lining and stitch the gap closed.

How much fabric do I need for a Christmas stocking?

About 1/2 yd of outer fabric and 1/2 yd of lining for a standard 18 in stocking from 44 in wide fabric, plus about 1/4 yd for the cuff. Mini stockings fit on a fat quarter. The template maker works it out for your size.

What size is a standard Christmas stocking?

About 18 in from the top to the heel, 7 in across the leg and 10 in from heel to toe. Large stockings are 20 to 22 in and mini ones about 7 in.

What fabric is best for Christmas stockings?

Quilting cotton is easiest to sew, with fusible fleece behind it for body. Velvet, faux fur and wool felt look cosy; felt and fleece don't fray, so they can be sewn without a lining.

How do you line a Christmas stocking?

Sew the lining pieces like the outer stocking, but leave a 4 in gap in the back seam. Put the right-side-out outer stocking inside the wrong-side-out lining, sew round the top, then pull everything through the gap and close it.

Can I sew a stocking by hand?

Yes. Use a small backstitch with doubled thread for the seams, 1/4 to 3/8 in from the edge. A felt stocking sewn with blanket stitch round the outside is the easiest hand-sewn version.
