Christmas crafts to make and sell (with prices that pay you)

19 Christmas crafts adults can make and sell, from crochet ornaments and beanies to candles and cards, each with rough material costs, making time and a price worked out for your own hourly rate.

Quick answer: the Christmas crafts that sell best are quick, giftable and easy to make in batches: crochet ornaments, beanies and ear warmers, mini stockings, gift bags, scrunchies, cards, candles and resin ornaments. Personalised makes (a name on a stocking or hoop) earn more. Price each one as materials + your hours × an hourly rate, plus about 10% for overheads and the selling fees: a beanie with $6 of yarn and 2½ hours at $15 an hour is about $54 on Etsy.

How to price Christmas crafts

  1. Materials: the share of yarn, fabric, wax or paper the item uses, plus labels and packaging.
  2. Time: your real hours times a rate you'd be happy to earn. Time yourself on the first one.
  3. Overheads: about 10% for tools, electricity, card fees and photos.
  4. Fees: on Etsy, the transaction and payment processing fees come out of each sale. The Etsy fee calculator shows the exact amount, and the craft fair profit calculator checks whether a booth fee is worth it.

If a price feels too high for your market, don't cut your rate: pick a faster make, use cheaper materials, or sell a set. Our full guide to pricing crochet items covers wholesale, discounts and the "materials × 3" trap.

What sells best at Christmas craft fairs

  • A spread of price points. Have something under $10 (ornaments, scrunchies, cards), a middle range ($20 to $40: beanies, sets of coasters, candles) and a few showpieces.
  • Sets and bundles. Three ornaments in a box are easier to buy as a gift than one.
  • Personalisation. Names on stockings and hoop ornaments. Take orders early and set a last order date.
  • Gift-ready packaging. A tag, a ribbon and a card back make a small item look like a present. Free printable Christmas gift tags help.
  • Stock planning: the craft fair checklist works out how many of each item to bring.

When to start making

Christmas shopping on Etsy starts early, and craft fairs run from mid November to mid December. Make stock in batches from early autumn, list online by early November, and stop taking custom orders when your making time runs out. Check whether your list fits with the handmade gift planner or the crochet time calculator. For gifts you're making for family rather than selling, see our Christmas crafts ideas.

Selling safely

  • Toys for babies and young children must meet toy safety rules where you sell (for example in the US, UK and EU): avoid small parts like buttons and loose safety eyes, or label items as decorations.
  • Candles need warning labels, and you should test your wax, wick and fragrance before selling.

Questions crafters ask

What are the best Christmas crafts to make and sell?

Quick, giftable makes that you can batch: crochet ornaments, beanies, ear warmers, mini stockings, gift bags, scrunchies, cards, candles and resin ornaments. Personalised items like name stockings sell for more.

How do I price handmade Christmas crafts?

Add materials and your hours times an hourly rate, then about 10% for overheads, then the selling fees. A $6-of-yarn beanie that takes 2.5 hours is about $47 before fees at $15 an hour.

What crafts can I make quickly to sell?

Scrunchies, drawstring gift bags, ear warmers, crochet ornaments, soy candles poured in batches and resin ornaments all take an hour or two or less each.

When should I start making crafts for Christmas sales?

Early autumn. Etsy Christmas shopping starts early and fairs run from mid November, so aim to have stock listed by early November.

Do I need to charge sales tax at a craft fair?

It depends on where you live. Many US states need a seller's permit even for one-day events; check your state or local tax office before the fair.

Is it worth selling crochet at Christmas?

Yes, if you price for your time. Choose faster makes (ornaments, ear warmers, chunky beanies) so the price stays one people will pay.
