Head ranges are from the Craft Yarn Council's head circumference chart. The typical head is the middle size that yarn companies and hat designers most often quote. Hat height runs from the bottom of an unfolded brim to the top of the crown on a fitted beanie, as listed by Knit Picks. Add 2 to 3 inches for a slouchy hat or a brim you fold up (our rule of thumb). Heads vary, so measure when you can.

How to measure a head for a hat

Wrap a soft tape measure around the head just above the eyebrows and ears, across the widest part at the back. Keep it level and comfortably snug, not tight. Making a surprise gift? Lay a hat the person already wears flat, without stretching it, measure across and double it.

Why a hat is smaller than the head

Knitted and crocheted fabric stretches, so a hat made the exact size of the head slides down over the eyes. Designers make the hat a little smaller, which is called negative ease. Most patterns use around 2 to 3 inches less than the head on an adult, or roughly 10 to 15 percent.

  • Ribbing pulls in and springs back, so ribbed hats can be made smaller.
  • Thick yarn and firm stitches stretch less, so they need less ease.
  • Crochet usually stretches less than knitting, so many crocheters make the band only a little smaller than the head. That's why the calculator suggests less ease for crochet.
  • Babies and children grow fast. When in doubt, go a little bigger.
Swatch in the round if you can. Measure your stitches over 4 inches (10 cm) after washing the swatch. Half a stitch per inch can change an adult hat by 2 inches.

Knitting a hat: the cast-on maths

Multiply the hat width by your stitches per inch, then round to a number that suits your rib and crown. For example, a 22 inch head with 12 percent ease gives a 19.4 inch hat. At 4.5 stitches per inch that's 87 stitches, so cast on 88. Knit until the hat is about 2.5 to 3 inches short of the full height, then start the crown decreases.

Crocheting a hat from the top down

Work a flat circle, increasing each round, until it measures the hat width divided by 3.14 across. Then stop increasing and work even rounds until the hat reaches the full height, measured from the centre of the crown. For a 19 inch hat that's a circle about 6 inches across.

Questions crafters ask

What size should an adult knit hat be?

Most adult heads are 21 to 24 inches around. A fitted beanie is usually made 2 to 3 inches smaller, so about 19 to 21 inches around, and about 11 inches tall before any fold-up brim.

How many stitches do I cast on for a hat?

Multiply the hat width (head size minus about 12 percent) by your stitches per inch, then round to suit your rib. In worsted yarn at 4.5 stitches per inch, an average adult hat is about 88 stitches.

How big should a crochet hat crown circle be?

Divide the hat width by 3.14. For an adult hat about 20 inches around, increase until the flat circle is about 6.4 inches across, then work even.

What size is a newborn hat?

A newborn head is about 14 inches around, so a newborn hat is usually about 12 to 13 inches around and 6 inches tall.

What is negative ease in a hat?

It means the hat is smaller than the head, so the stretchy fabric holds it in place. Hats usually have about 10 to 15 percent negative ease, more for ribbing and less for thick or firm fabric.
