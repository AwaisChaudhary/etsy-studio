Row counter

Tap the big button each time you finish a row. Add a target, count pattern repeats, and run as many counters as your project needs. Everything saves on this device.

Keyboard: press Space or + to add a row on the first counter, − to take one away.

Quick answer: tap the big + button after every row (or press Space on a keyboard). Set "Repeat every" to the length of your pattern repeat, add a target if you know the total, and tick "Keep screen on" so the phone doesn't lock mid-row. Counts save on this device automatically.

How do I use the repeat counter?

Many patterns repeat a set of rows, like "repeat rows 1 to 8 six times". Put 8 in "Repeat every". The counter then shows which row of the repeat you're on and adds one to your repeat count each time you finish the set.

Use a second counter for something separate, like decreases on a sleeve or rounds on a hat crown, so you never have to keep two numbers in your head.

How do I keep track of increase and decrease rows?

Patterns often say something like "decrease at each end every 6th row, 8 times". Make a second counter called "Decreases", set its repeat to 6, and give it a target of 48 (6 × 8). It tells you which row of the six you're on, and adds one to the repeat count each time you reach a decrease row. Your main counter keeps the total rows.

Need to work out the spacing first, like "increase 14 stitches evenly across the row"? The increase and decrease evenly calculator writes the row out for you.

How do I count rows I've already knitted?

  • Knitting: each V in a column of stockinette is one row. Count the Vs from just above the cast-on edge up to the needle; the loops on the needle are the current row, not yet counted.
  • Garter stitch: each ridge is two rows.
  • Crochet: count the tall stitch posts in one column; for single crochet, each little ridge of loops is one row.

Tap "Set number" on the counter, type the rows you counted, then carry on tapping.

Questions crafters ask

How do I stop my phone screen turning off while I knit?

Tick 'Keep screen on'. It uses your browser's screen wake feature, which works in most up-to-date browsers on Android, iPhone and computers. It switches off when you leave the page.

Does the row counter work offline?

Once the page has loaded it keeps working without a connection, and your counts are saved on your device.

Can I count rounds instead of rows?

Yes. Rename the counter to 'Rounds' by tapping its name. It counts the same way.

How do I count decrease rows like every 6th row?

Add a second counter, set its repeat to 6 and its target to 6 times the number of decreases. It shows which row of the six you're on and counts each completed repeat, while your first counter keeps the total.

How do I count rows in knitting?

In stockinette, count the Vs in one column from above the cast-on edge; each V is one row. In garter stitch each ridge is two rows. Don't count the loops on your needle.
