Etsy title and tag checker

Paste your listing title and tags. See what's over Etsy's limits, what's repeated and which tags are wasting a slot.

Quick answer: Etsy allows a title of up to 140 characters and 13 tags of up to 20 characters each. Use all 13 tags, make them multi-word phrases buyers would type, and don't spend tags repeating your category or exact title words unless they're part of a phrase. Put the clearest description of the item at the start of the title.

How to write Etsy tags that get found

  • Use all 13. Each tag is another search your listing can match.
  • Use phrases, not single words. "Nail price list" matches a real search; "nail" on its own competes with millions of listings.
  • Think like the buyer. Who it's for ("gift for crocheter"), what it does ("pricing calculator"), the format ("google sheets"), the occasion.
  • Don't waste tags on your category or attributes. Etsy already uses your category and attributes (colour, occasion) for search, so tags are better spent on other phrases.
  • Check spelling variants only when buyers really use them ("colour" and "color" if you sell to both the UK and US).

How to write an Etsy title in 2026

Lead with what the item is in plain words, then the most important details. Etsy has encouraged shorter, clearer titles for several years, so a long list of keywords is no longer the advantage it once was: put the extra search phrases in your tags and description instead.

Questions crafters ask

How many characters can an Etsy title have?

Up to 140 characters.

How many tags can you use on Etsy?

13 tags, each up to 20 characters.

Should Etsy tags be single words or phrases?

Phrases usually work better: they match what buyers actually type and face less competition than single words.

Do Etsy tags need to repeat the title?

No. Etsy matches searches across your title, tags, category and attributes, so use tags for phrases your title doesn't already cover.
