Basic crochet stitches for beginners

The six stitches nearly every crochet pattern is built from: chain, slip stitch, single, half double, double and treble crochet. Step through each one in our drawings, and switch between US and UK names with one tap.

Quick answer: the basic crochet stitches, shortest to tallest, are the chain (ch), slip stitch (sl st), single crochet (sc), half double crochet (hdc), double crochet (dc) and treble crochet (tr). Each taller stitch starts with one more yarn over, and needs one more turning chain: 1 for sc, 2 for hdc, 3 for dc and 4 for tr. Learn the chain and single crochet first; the rest are the same moves with extra yarn overs.

Where do I put the hook? Front loop, back loop and post

The top of every stitch looks like a sideways V, or a chain lying on its side. Unless the pattern says otherwise, put the hook under both loops of the V. Going into only the back loop (BLO) leaves a ridge that makes stretchy ribbing; the front loop (FLO) leaves a ridge on the other side. Working around the post, the upright body of the stitch, makes raised textures like front and back post double crochet.

How to chain (ch)

  1. Slip knot on the hook. Make a slip knot and put it on the hook. It isn't counted as a chain.
  2. Yarn over. Wrap the yarn over the hook from back to front.
  3. Pull through. Pull the wrapped yarn through the loop on the hook. That's 1 chain. Repeat for as many as you need.
  4. Keep going. Count the V shapes, not the loop on the hook. Keep them loose and even so the next row goes in easily.

Starting chains, spaces in lace and granny squares, turning chains.

How to slip stitch (sl st)

  1. Insert the hook. Put the hook into the stitch, under both top loops.
  2. Yarn over. Wrap the yarn over the hook.
  3. Pull through everything. Pull the yarn through the stitch and the loop on the hook in one go. Done: the flattest stitch, used to join rounds and move along an edge.

Joining rounds, moving along an edge, a firm edging.

How to single crochet (sc)

  1. Insert the hook. Put the hook into the stitch, under both top loops.
  2. Yarn over and pull up a loop. Wrap the yarn and pull it back through the stitch.
  3. Yarn over. Wrap the yarn over the hook again.
  4. Pull through both loops. Pull through the 2 loops on the hook. One stitch done, 1 loop left.

Amigurumi, dishcloths, bags, firm dense fabric. Turning chain: ch 1.

How to half double crochet (hdc)

  1. Yarn over first. Wrap the yarn over the hook before you go into the stitch.
  2. Insert the hook. Put the hook into the next stitch.
  3. Yarn over and pull up a loop. Wrap and pull back through the stitch. You have 3 loops.
  4. Yarn over, pull through all 3. Wrap once more and pull through all 3 loops at once. Done.

Hats, blankets, a warm fabric that works up quicker than sc. Turning chain: ch 2.

How to double crochet (dc)

  1. Yarn over first. Wrap the yarn over the hook once.
  2. Insert the hook. Put the hook into the next stitch.
  3. Yarn over and pull up a loop. Wrap and pull back through the stitch. You have 3 loops.
  4. Yarn over, pull through 2. Wrap and pull through the first 2 loops only. 2 loops left.
  5. Yarn over, pull through the last 2. Wrap and pull through the last 2 loops. Done.

Blankets, granny squares, shawls: fast and drapey. Turning chain: ch 3.

How to treble crochet (tr)

  1. Yarn over twice. Wrap the yarn over the hook two times.
  2. Insert the hook. Put the hook into the next stitch.
  3. Yarn over and pull up a loop. Wrap and pull back through the stitch. You have 4 loops.
  4. Yarn over, pull through 2. Wrap and pull through the first 2 loops only. 3 loops left.
  5. Yarn over, pull through 2 again. Wrap and pull through the next 2 loops. 2 loops left.
  6. Yarn over, pull through the last 2. Wrap and pull through the last 2 loops: one treble finished. It's the tallest of the basic stitches.

Lace, tall rows, fast airy blankets. Turning chain: ch 4.

Does the turning chain count as a stitch?

For single crochet, no: chain 1, turn, and work into the first stitch. For taller stitches it depends on the pattern. Many count the chain 3 of a double crochet row as the first stitch, so you skip the first stitch and work the last stitch into the top of the turning chain. Others say "chain 2 (does not count)". Check the note at the start of the pattern, and count your stitches at the end of the first few rows. More on stars, brackets and stitch counts in how to read crochet patterns.

Why is my crochet getting wider or narrower?

Almost always a stitch gained or lost at the edges. A stitch added in the first stitch (when the turning chain also counts) or one missed at the end (the top of the turning chain is easy to miss) changes the count each row. Put a stitch marker in the first and last stitch of every row, count after each row for a while, and use a row counter to keep your place.

Why are US and UK crochet terms different?

They count differently. US names count the pull-throughs that finish the stitch after you pull up a loop; UK names count the loops on the hook right after you pull up that first loop. So the same stitch is a US single crochet and a UK double crochet. A pattern with "sc" anywhere in it is US: there is no sc in UK terms. Our US vs UK crochet terms chart has the full list.

Questions crafters ask

What are the basic crochet stitches?

Chain (ch), slip stitch (sl st), single crochet (sc), half double crochet (hdc), double crochet (dc) and treble crochet (tr), in US terms. Almost every other stitch is a mix of these.

What is the easiest crochet stitch for beginners?

The chain, then single crochet. Single crochet is just insert, pull up a loop, and pull through two, so it's the best first stitch for practising even tension.

How many chains do you need to turn for each crochet stitch?

Usually 1 for single crochet, 2 for half double, 3 for double and 4 for treble crochet (US terms). Some patterns use one fewer, so check yours.

Is UK double crochet the same as US single crochet?

Yes. UK double crochet (dc) is US single crochet (sc), UK treble (tr) is US double crochet (dc), and UK half treble (htr) is US half double crochet (hdc).

How many loops should be on my hook?

After a finished stitch, 1. Mid-stitch: 2 for single crochet, 3 for half double and double crochet, 4 for treble crochet, right after you pull up the loop through the stitch.

How tall is a double crochet compared to a single crochet?

About twice as tall. A half double is about one and a half times, and a treble about two and a half to three times, depending on your yarn and tension.
