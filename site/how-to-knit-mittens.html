How to knit mittens (easy pattern for any size)

Mittens are a hat-sized project with one extra trick: the thumb. This beginner guide shows each step in drawings, and the pattern writer gives your cast-on, every gusset increase, the hand length and the top decreases for any size and yarn.

Quick answer: to knit mittens, cast on about as many stitches as your hand circumference times your gauge (36 stitches for a women's medium in worsted), join in the round and knit a 2.5 in rib cuff. Then make a thumb gusset by increasing 2 stitches every third round, put those stitches on waste yarn, and knit the hand straight until it reaches the tip of your little finger. Decrease 4 stitches a round to close the top, then pick up the thumb stitches and knit the thumb. A pair of adult worsted mittens takes roughly 140 to 200 yards.

How to knit mittens, step by step

What you need

  • Yarn: wool or a wool blend is warmest and stays warm when damp. Worsted (4) is the easiest to start with. Pick a smooth, light colour so you can see your stitches.
  • Needles: a set of double-pointed needles (dpns) or one long circular needle (32 in / 80 cm) for the magic loop, one size smaller than the yarn label suggests so the fabric is dense and windproof. About 4 to 4.5 mm (US 6 to 7) for worsted.
  • Also: 2 stitch markers, a piece of smooth waste yarn, scissors and a tapestry needle.

New to knitting in the round? See how to knit in the round and how to increase (the gusset uses M1L and M1R) before you start. The pick up stitches guide helps at the thumb.

Mitten size chart

Measure round the widest part of the hand, over the knuckles, without the thumb. Then measure from the wrist crease to the tip of the middle finger. These are typical measurements; a quick tape measure beats any chart.

Mittens are knitted with little or no ease: the same size as the hand, or a touch bigger. A snug mitten is warmer; too loose and the cold gets in at the cuff.

How the thumb gusset works

Your hand is wider across the base of the thumb than at the wrist. A gusset is a little triangle of extra stitches that grows from the cuff to the thumb joint. You make it by increasing 2 stitches every third round between two markers. When it holds about a third of the hand stitches, those stitches go on waste yarn and become the thumb later. A mitten without a gusset (a straight "peasant thumb") is quicker but pulls across the palm.

Fixing holes at the thumb

The gap where the thumb meets the hand is the one place mittens get holes. Pick up 2 stitches (or even 3) from the gap instead of 1, and on the next round knit 2 of them together. Pull the first stitch on each needle snug. Any small hole left can be closed with the tail when you weave in the ends: see how to weave in ends.

Flat mittens (two needles)

If you don't want to knit in the round yet, you can knit mittens flat on two straight needles and sew them up the side. Use the same stitch counts plus 2 for the seam, work the gusset on one side of the piece, and seam with mattress stitch. Round mittens have no seam to rub, which is why most patterns use them.

Making a pair

Write the round count when you start the gusset and when you finish the hand on the first mitten. Our row counter keeps track. The two mittens in this pattern are the same, so you can wear them on either hand. Want matching gifts? Add a knitted hat and scarf from the same yarn.

Pattern, drawings and wording are our own, using standard top-down-from-cuff mitten construction (rib cuff, M1 thumb gusset every third round, band decreases), checked against Yarnspirations, Gathered and Handy Little Me beginner mittens (28 Nov 2026). Hand sizes are typical figures. Gauges are firm versions of the Craft Yarn Council ranges; round counts, lengths and yardage are estimates, so try the mitten on as you go.

Questions crafters ask

Are mittens easy to knit for beginners?

Yes, if you can knit, purl, knit in the round and make an increase. Mittens are small, so they're quick, and the thumb gusset is the only new skill.

How many stitches do I cast on for mittens?

Multiply your hand circumference by your stitches per inch and round to a multiple of 4. A women's medium hand (7.5 in) in worsted at 5 stitches per inch needs about 36 to 40 stitches.

How much yarn do I need for mittens?

A pair of adult mittens in worsted takes roughly 140 to 200 yards; children's mittens 70 to 120 yards. Long cuffs and chunky yarn use more. The writer above estimates it for your size.

What needles are best for knitting mittens?

Double-pointed needles or a long circular needle used for the magic loop, one size smaller than the yarn label suggests, so the mitten is dense and warm.

Can you knit mittens on straight needles?

Yes. Knit the mitten flat with 2 extra stitches for the seam, shape the thumb on one edge, and sew it up the side with mattress stitch.

How long should a mitten be?

From the wrist, the hand part should reach the tip of the little finger before the top decreases start. With the decreases, the mitten covers the fingertips with a little room to spare.
