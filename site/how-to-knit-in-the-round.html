How to knit in the round

Hats, cowls, socks and sweater bodies are knitted in a tube with no seam. Here's how to join without twisting in four drawings, which circular needle length you need, and a finder that picks the needle for your project.

Quick answer: cast on onto a circular needle shorter than your project's circumference (a 16 in / 40 cm circular for an adult hat), spread the stitches round the cable and check the cast-on edge sits on the inside of the ring with no twist. Put a marker on the right tip, then knit the first stitch on the left tip with the working yarn and pull it snug. Knitting every round makes stockinette. For socks, sleeves and hat crowns too small for any circular, use magic loop on a 32 in (80 cm) or longer needle, or double-pointed needles.

What length circular needle do I need?

Pick a circular that is shorter than the circumference of what you're knitting, so the stitches reach from tip to tip without stretching (Knit Picks). Length is measured from tip to tip, not just the cable (Elizabeth Smith Knits). A needle that's a bit short is fine: the stitches bunch up on the cable. One that's too long stretches them and they won't meet.

How do you join in the round without twisting?

Before the first stitch, lay the needle flat and run your finger along the cast-on edge. It should sit on the inside of the ring (or all along the bottom of the needle) the whole way round, never spiralling over the cable (Knit Picks). Then join with the working yarn. If counting a long cast-on gets confusing, Cocoknits knits one row flat first: a flat row makes the edge easy to see, and the small gap is sewn shut with the tail later.

I twisted my join. Can I fix it?

If you haven't knitted a full round yet, spin the stitches round the needle until the edge lies flat on the inside, then join (Berroco). Once you've knitted a round or more, the twist is locked in: Knit Picks says it can't be corrected later, so rip back and cast on again. (A twisted tube becomes a Möbius loop, which some knitters make on purpose for cowls, but not for a hat.)

How do I close the gap at the join?

A little ladder at the join is normal. Our tips: pull the first two or three stitches snug, or use the swap or extra-stitch join above. Any gap left over is closed when you weave in the cast-on tail: take it across the gap to the stitch on the other side, then weave it in as in our weaving in ends guide.

What if my project is smaller than any circular?

Socks, mittens, sleeves and the top of a hat get too small for even a 16 in needle. You have three choices:

  • Magic loop: one long circular, 32 in (80 cm) or 40 in (100 cm), with the extra cable pulled out as a loop at two points so half the stitches sit on each tip.
  • Double-pointed needles (DPNs): the stitches are shared over 3 or 4 short needles and you knit with a spare one.
  • Two circulars: half the stitches on each needle, each knitted with its own tips.

For a hat, start on a 16 in circular and change to magic loop or DPNs when the crown decreases make the stitches stretch.

Is knitting in the round the same as knitting flat?

The stitches are the same, but you never turn the work, so the right side always faces you. That changes some stitch patterns:

  • Stockinette: knit every round (flat, you knit one row and purl the next).
  • Garter stitch: knit one round, purl one round.
  • Rib: knit the knits and purl the purls, every round. The stitch count must be a multiple of the rib (4 for k2, p2).

Rounds spiral, so stripes show a small step at the start of the round. Our tip for a neater stripe: on the second round of a new colour, slip the first stitch purlwise, then knit on. Keep count of rounds with the row counter.

Questions crafters ask

How do you join knitting in the round?

Cast on onto a circular needle shorter than the project, spread the stitches round the cable and check the cast-on edge sits on the inside with no twist. Place a marker on the right tip, then knit the first stitch on the left tip with the working yarn and pull it snug.

What size circular needle do I need for a hat?

A 16 inch (40 cm) circular for an adult or child hat. Change to magic loop or double-pointed needles when the crown gets too small. A 24 inch needle is too long for most hats.

Can I knit a hat on a 24 inch circular needle?

Usually not. Most adult hats are about 20 to 22 inches around, shorter than the needle, so the stitches won't stretch round it. Use a 16 inch circular, or magic loop on a 32 inch or longer needle.

How do I fix a twisted join in the round?

If you haven't knitted a full round, spin the stitches until the edge lies flat on the inside and then join. After a full round the twist can't be undone, so rip back and cast on again.

How do you knit stockinette in the round?

Knit every round. Because you never turn the work, the right side always faces you, so there are no purl rows.

What is magic loop?

A way to knit a small tube on one long circular needle (32 in / 80 cm or longer): the extra cable is pulled out as a loop at two points so half the stitches sit on each tip.
