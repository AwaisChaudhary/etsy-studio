How to crochet a magic ring

The magic ring (or magic circle) starts a round without a hole in the middle. Here it is in six drawings, with a helper that writes your first rounds for you.

Quick answer: make a loop with the tail crossing over the working yarn, put the hook through, pull up a loop of the working yarn and chain 1 (it doesn't count). Work 6 single crochet into the ring, going under both the ring and the tail, then pull the tail to close the hole. Join with a slip stitch to the first stitch, or keep going in a spiral. For half double crochet start with ch 2 and 8 hdc; for double crochet, ch 3 and 10 to 12 dc.

Our own drawings, shown for right-handed crochet. Left-handed? It's the same steps mirrored: the tail sits on the other side.

Common starting counts from Sarah Maker, Love Life Yarn and B.Hooked. Patterns vary from about 3 to 12 or more stitches, so follow yours. To keep a circle flat, add the first round's count again every round; the crochet circle calculator writes every round out to the size you want.

Which strand do I pull to close a magic ring?

The tail: the short end you started with. The working yarn runs to the ball. If you pull and nothing moves, you're pulling the working yarn, or your stitches went over the ring but not over the tail. Every stitch in round 1 has to go under both strands, or the tail can't slide.

Why won't my magic ring close?

  • The tail wasn't worked over. Look at the back: the tail should disappear inside the stitches all the way round.
  • The stitches are gripping the tail. Hold round 1 a little looser, or pull the tail a bit before you finish the round so it's already sliding.
  • Fuzzy or sticky yarn. Chenille, velvet and mohair can grab or snap. Make round 1 loose, or make the ring in a smooth cotton and change yarn after it.
  • Too many stitches. A ring of 12 or more tall stitches can't fully close. A small hole is normal for a dc ring.

How do I stop a magic ring coming undone?

Leave a tail of at least 6 in (15 cm). When the piece is done, thread the tail on a yarn needle, pull the ring closed once more, and weave the tail in round the back of round 1, then back the other way through a few stitches so it can't slide out. Don't trim it short until the end. A knot isn't needed, and it can make a lump on the right side.

What's the difference between a magic ring and a magic circle?

None: magic ring, magic circle, magic loop and adjustable ring are all names for the same start. A double magic ring is the same idea with the yarn wrapped twice, so there are two strands to work over. It's even more secure, and closing it takes two pulls, one for each strand.

What can I use instead of a magic ring?

  • Chain 2, then work your first round into the 2nd chain from the hook. Easy, though it leaves a small hole and can't be tightened later.
  • Chain 4 and join with a slip stitch to make a ring, then work into the ring. This leaves a clear hole, which is fine for granny squares and mandalas.

Joined rounds or a spiral?

Joined rounds end with a slip stitch into the first stitch and start with a chain. They make a neat line of joins and stripes that stay straight: good for coasters, hats and granny circles. Spiral rounds just keep going, so there's no seam, which is why amigurumi patterns use them. Questions crafters ask

How do you crochet a magic ring?

Make a loop with the tail crossing over the working yarn, put the hook through, pull up a loop of the working yarn and chain 1. Work your stitches into the ring, under both the ring and the tail, then pull the tail to close the hole.

How many stitches go in a magic ring?

Usually 6 single crochet. Half double crochet rings often start with 8, and double crochet with 10 to 12. Your pattern may say otherwise, so follow it.

Does the chain 1 count as a stitch in a magic ring?

No. The ch 1 for single crochet just holds the ring. For taller stitches, some patterns count the ch 3 as the first double crochet and some don't, so check yours.

Which end do you pull to close a magic ring?

The tail, the short end you started with. If it won't move, your stitches didn't go over the tail, or you're pulling the working yarn.

Why does my magic ring keep coming open?

The tail wasn't woven in firmly. Pull the ring tight again, then weave the tail in round the back of round 1 and back the other way so it can't slide out.

Is a magic circle the same as a magic ring?

Yes. Magic ring, magic circle, magic loop and adjustable ring are different names for the same start.
