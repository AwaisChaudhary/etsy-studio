How to pick up stitches in knitting

Picking up stitches adds a new piece of knitting straight onto an edge, for a neckband, a button band or a border, with no sewing. Here's how in four drawings, the usual ratios, and a calculator that tells you how many to pick up and how to space them.

Quick answer: with the right side facing, push the needle through the fabric one full stitch in from the edge, wrap the yarn as if to knit and pull a loop through. Each loop is one new stitch. On the side of stockinette or rib, pick up 3 stitches for every 4 rows (or 2 for every 3), so 80 rows gives 60 stitches. On garter stitch pick up 1 per ridge. Along a cast on or bound off edge pick up 1 in every stitch. If your pattern gives a number, follow it and spread the stitches evenly with markers.

How many stitches do I pick up?

Knitted stitches are wider than they are tall, so there are more rows than stitches in an inch. If you picked up one stitch for every row on a side edge, the band would have too many stitches and ripple. That's why the usual ratios skip a row now and then:

Side edge of stockinette, rib or seed3 stitches for every 4 rows (or 2 for every 3)Wikipedia, Knotions, Purl Soho, Interweave
Side edge of garter stitch1 stitch per garter ridge (1 for every 2 rows)Wikipedia, Purl Soho
Cast on or bound off edge1 stitch in every stitchWikipedia, Knotions, Quince & Co.
Neckline curve or diagonalEach part on its own: 1 per stitch on flat parts, 3 for 4 rows on straight sides, between those on slopesInterweave, Purl Soho
Loose or wide-gauge edgeTry 4 for every 5 rows if 3 for 4 looks gappyLoveCrafts, Purl Soho

Purl Soho says to choose between 3 for 4 and 2 for 3 by your gauge. Your pattern's number always wins over a rule of thumb.

Where exactly do I put the needle?

  • Side edges: between the edge stitch and the next one in (Quince & Co., Interweave). Going a full stitch in hides the wobbly edge stitch on the wrong side.
  • Cast on or bound off edges: into the middle of the V of each stitch just below the edge, one new stitch per stitch (Quince & Co., Interweave). Going into the centre lines the new band up with the columns below.
  • Curves and slopes: one full stitch in from the shaped edge, and not into the little holes where bound off steps meet (Purl Soho).

What's the difference between "pick up" and "pick up and knit"?

Most patterns mean the same thing: draw a loop of working yarn through the edge, which puts a new stitch on your needle. Some knitters pick up all the loops first and then knit them, others knit each one as it's picked up (Wikipedia). LoveCrafts draws a small line between them: with "pick up" the new loops wait on the needle to be knitted on the next row, with "pick up and knit" each loop is knitted as you go. If your pattern says "pick up and knit", use the working yarn as in the drawings. The row you pick up is not usually counted as the band's first row.

How do I space picked-up stitches evenly?

  1. Divide the edge in half and then in quarters with locking stitch markers (Interweave, Purl Soho).
  2. Divide your stitch count by the number of sections. The calculator above does it and lists rows and stitches for each section.
  3. Pick up that many in each section, using a steady rhythm such as "pick up 3, skip 1". Don't skip two rows in a row (Quince & Co.).
  4. Count at each marker, so a mistake costs you one section, not the whole edge.

How do I pick up stitches around a neckline?

Treat each part of the neck as its own edge (Interweave): the bound off stitches at the centre front and back neck (1 per stitch), the straight sides (3 for 4 rows), and the decreased slopes in between. Put markers where each part changes, mirror the counts on both sides, and start at a shoulder seam or the centre back. Necklines are usually picked up in the round on a 16 in circular needle; see how to knit in the round.

Why do I get holes, and how do I stop them?

  • Use a smaller needle for the pick-up row (Interweave, Purl Soho), then switch to the band's needle on the next row.
  • Don't pick up into existing holes where bound off steps meet the slope; go one stitch in.
  • Pick up an extra stitch at a gap and knit it together with its neighbour on the next row (Knotions).
  • Plain edges pick up best. Slipped-stitch edges have one edge stitch for every two rows, which leaves fewer places to pick up and can make gaps (Interweave, Purl Soho).
  • Snug the first stitch: after a round, give the tail a gentle tug (Purl Soho), then weave it in, see weaving in ends.

What if I picked up the wrong number?

A few stitches out is fine. On the first row of the band, k2tog or p2tog to lose a stitch, or work into the front and back of a stitch to add one (Interweave). Spread the fixes out, as the increase and decrease evenly calculator shows. If the band ripples or pulls in a lot, pull it out and pick up again: it's only one row.

Questions crafters ask

How many stitches do I pick up along a side edge?

Usually 3 stitches for every 4 rows on stockinette, rib or seed stitch (or 2 for every 3 rows), so 80 rows gives 60 stitches. On garter stitch pick up 1 stitch per ridge. Follow your pattern's number if it gives one.

How do you pick up stitches in knitting?

With the right side facing, put the needle through the fabric one full stitch in from the edge, wrap the yarn as if to knit and pull a loop through. Each loop is a new stitch. Skip a row now and then on side edges so the band lies flat.

How many stitches do I pick up along a cast on or bound off edge?

One in every stitch, going into the centre of each V just below the edge.

Is the pick up row counted as the first row?

Not usually. The row you pick up puts stitches on the needle; the band's first row is the next one you work. Check your pattern.

How do I avoid holes when picking up stitches?

Pick up with a smaller needle, go one full stitch in from the edge, avoid the holes where bound off steps meet a slope, and pick up an extra stitch at a gap and knit it together with its neighbour on the next row.

What does 'pick up and knit 3 stitches for every 4 rows' mean?

Pull a new loop through the edge in 3 rows, skip the 4th row, and repeat along the edge.
