How to weave in ends

Every project finishes with tails to hide. Here's how to fasten off in crochet, weave in ends in crochet and knitting so they never poke out, what to do with slippery and chunky yarn, and a helper that tells you how long to leave, how far to weave and how long it'll take.

Quick answer: leave a tail of about 6 in (15 cm) (8 in for chunky yarn). Thread it on a blunt tapestry needle and, on the wrong side, run it through about 1 to 2 in (3 to 5 cm) of stitches of the same colour. Then change direction and go back through different strands; turn a third time for blankets and things that get washed a lot. Gently stretch the fabric and trim close. Weave ends in before blocking, and skip the knots.

How long should the tail be to weave in?

YarnTail to leaveHow far to weave
Wool and other grippy yarn6 in (15 cm)3-5 stitches each way, 2 directions
Cotton, acrylic and smooth yarn6 in (15 cm)5-7 stitches each way, split the plies
Slippery yarn: superwash, silk, bamboo6-8 in (15-20 cm)5-7 stitches, 3 directions; sewing thread for extra hold
Bulky and super bulky8 in (20 cm)Split the plies and weave each one separately
Chenille, velvet and T-shirt yarn8 in (20 cm)Can't be split: pull through with a small crochet hook

Tail lengths from Make and Do Crew, MJ's Off The Hook Designs and Yarnspirations (6 in is the common minimum; thicker yarn needs more). Stitch counts from Cocoknits' duplicate stitch guide. The 8 in for bulky yarn is our rounding.

How do I fasten off in crochet?

  1. Finish the last stitch. Chain 1 if your pattern says so.
  2. Cut the yarn, leaving about 6 in (15 cm).
  3. Yarn over and pull the cut end all the way through the loop on the hook.
  4. Tug the tail gently to close the loop. Not so hard that the last stitch puckers.

What is an invisible fasten off?

On joined rounds, a normal fasten off leaves a little bump at the join. For a smoother finish, skip the slip stitch join: cut the yarn after the last stitch and pull the tail straight up through the last stitch. Thread it on a tapestry needle, pass it under both top loops of the first stitch of the round (skip the turning chain), then go back down into the centre of the last stitch. It makes a stitch that looks like all the others. Weave in on the wrong side.

How do I weave in ends in crochet?

Turn the work to the wrong side. Run the tail through the posts or along the tops of 4 to 6 stitches, about 1 to 2 in. Turn and go back the other way, skipping one strand, so the tail zigzags. MJ's Off The Hook Designs weaves in at least two directions and adds a third for things that get heavy use. On granny squares, Make and Do Crew weave under the thick clusters and change direction once. For a magic ring, weave round the first-round stitches, then reverse and tug: it pulls the centre closed too.

How do I weave in ends in knitting?

  • Duplicate stitch (Swiss darning): on the purl side, follow the path of the yarn through the stitches. It holds better than a straight line and doesn't show on the front (Cocoknits).
  • Along a seam or edge: if there's a seam or a selvedge, weave up through it, then back.
  • In ribbing: weave up and down inside one column of stitches so the rib can still stretch.
  • Cast-on and bind-off tails: weave along the edge for a few stitches, then turn into the fabric. At the top of a hat, thread the tail through the live stitches, pull them closed and weave in on the inside.

Should I tie a knot before weaving in?

No. Knots can work loose and poke through to the front, and you can feel them in a garment. A well woven-in tail holds better. Make and Do Crew also say not to use glue or a lighter; a fabric-safe glue is fine only as an extra layer on something decorative.

How do I weave in slippery or chunky yarn?

For slippery yarn (superwash, silk, bamboo, some cottons), weave further, 5 to 7 stitches each way, split the plies on the way back, and make three turns. Cocoknits' extra trick: whipstitch the tails together with sewing thread, then knot and clip the thread, not the yarn. For chunky yarn, split the plies and weave each one on its own so there's no lump. Chenille, velvet and T-shirt yarn can't be split, so pull them through with a small crochet hook (Make and Do Crew). Fuzzy yarn like mohair grabs on to itself, so a short weave holds well, but it's hard to undo, so weave in carefully the first time.

How do I hide ends in amigurumi?

After fastening off, close any opening by sewing through the front loops of the last round and pulling tight. Then push the needle into the toy, through the stuffing and out the other side. Pull the tail a little so the toy squashes, snip it close and let go: the end disappears inside.

Should I weave in ends before or after blocking?

Before. Yarnspirations and Make and Do Crew both weave in ends before blocking or seaming, so blocking sets the tails in place with the stitches. Some knitters leave the last bit untrimmed until after blocking, then snip it, so the tail can't pull back through when the fabric relaxes. Next step: how to block knitting and crochet.

How do I weave in fewer ends?

  • Crochet over the tails: lay them along the top of the row and work the next few stitches over them.
  • Weave in as you go on big or many-colour projects, so you don't face a pile at the end (Make and Do Crew).
  • Carry yarn up the side for even-row stripes instead of cutting. Our stripe planner counts the ends for you.
  • Join same-colour yarn with a Russian join or spit splice (for wool that felts), which leaves no ends at all.

Questions crafters ask

How long should a tail be to weave in?

About 6 in (15 cm) for most yarn. Leave about 8 in (20 cm) for chunky, slippery or chenille yarn.

How do you weave in ends in crochet?

On the wrong side, run the tail through the posts of a few stitches for about 1 to 2 in, then turn and go back the other way through different strands. Add a third direction for blankets. Stretch gently and trim.

How do you weave in ends in knitting?

On the purl side, follow the path of the yarn through 3 to 5 stitches (duplicate stitch), then come back the other way, splitting the plies. Use 5 to 7 stitches for slippery yarn.

Should I tie a knot before weaving in ends?

No. Knots can work loose and poke through. Weaving in two or three directions holds better.

Do you weave in ends before or after blocking?

Before. Blocking then sets the tails in place. Some knitters trim the last bit after blocking.

How do you fasten off in crochet?

Cut the yarn leaving about 6 in (15 cm), yarn over and pull the cut end all the way through the loop on the hook, then tug gently.

How do you stop ends coming out of slippery yarn?

Weave further (5 to 7 stitches each way), split the plies, change direction three times, and for extra hold whipstitch the tails together with sewing thread.
