Embroidery pattern generator (printable, free)

Floral hoop designs drawn by our code, sized to your hoop. Print, trace onto fabric and stitch.

Quick answer: choose your hoop size, then print at actual size and trace the design onto fabric with a water-soluble pen over a light box or window. Beginners can stitch the whole design in backstitch, lazy daisy and French knots.

Print at "Actual size" (not "Fit to page").

Preview

How to transfer an embroidery pattern

All five methods, with drawings and a picker for your fabric, are on how to transfer an embroidery pattern.

  • Light box or window: tape the printout behind the fabric and trace with a water-soluble or heat-erasable pen.
  • Dark fabric: print on water-soluble stabiliser, stick it on, stitch through it, then rinse it away.
  • Carbon transfer paper: place it between pattern and fabric and trace firmly with a stylus.

Stitches for this pattern

  • Backstitch or stem stitch for stems and the wreath line.
  • Lazy daisy for leaves and five-petal flowers.
  • Satin stitch to fill small leaves.
  • French knots for flower centres and the dotted flowers.

Supplies

Use cotton or linen in a hoop one size bigger than the design, 6-strand embroidery floss split into 2 or 3 strands, and a size 5 to 9 embroidery needle. New to the stitches? Questions crafters ask

How do I transfer an embroidery pattern to fabric?

Trace it with a water-soluble pen over a light box or bright window, or print it on water-soluble stabiliser for dark fabric.

What stitches do beginners need for floral embroidery?

Backstitch or stem stitch, lazy daisy, satin stitch and French knots cover most floral patterns.

Are these embroidery patterns free to use?

Yes, for personal projects and gifts. Every design is drawn by our own code.
