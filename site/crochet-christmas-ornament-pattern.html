Free crochet Christmas ornament pattern (easy bauble)

A round crochet bauble is the easiest Christmas ornament you can make: it's one stuffed ball in single crochet with a little gold cap and a hanging loop. Pick a size and a stripe style below and the pattern rewrites itself, round by round, with tick boxes so you never lose your place.

Quick answer: to crochet an easy Christmas bauble, work 6 sc in a magic ring, increase by 6 stitches every round to 30 (5 rounds), work 5 plain rounds (switch colour in the middle for a band), then decrease by 6 every round back to 6, stuffing as you go. Add a 12-stitch gold cap and a 20-chain loop. In worsted yarn with a 3.5 mm hook it's about 2 in (5 cm) across and uses about 15 yd of yarn (our estimate). Most people finish one in 45 to 60 minutes.

How to crochet a Christmas bauble, step by step

What you need

  • Yarn: a smooth acrylic or cotton in Christmas colours, plus a scrap of gold or silver (a metallic yarn or embroidery thread held together with yarn looks lovely). One ball makes lots: a medium bauble takes about 15 yards.
  • Hook: about two sizes smaller than the yarn label suggests, so the stitches are tight and the stuffing doesn't show (3.5 mm for worsted).
  • Also: a stitch marker, a little polyester toy stuffing, a tapestry needle and scissors. Ribbon or baker's twine works instead of a chain loop.

Abbreviations

sc single crochet (UK double crochet, dc), inc 2 sc in the same stitch, dec sc 2 together (the invisible decrease through the front loops looks neatest), BLO back loop only, sl st slip stitch. The number in brackets is the stitch count at the end of the round. Switch the writer to UK terms and every stitch name changes for you. Colours are called A (main) and B (contrast); change colour on the last pull-through of the round before, as in our guide to changing colours in crochet.

The cap and hanging loop

  1. With gold yarn, make a magic ring and work 6 sc into it (6).
  2. Inc in each stitch around (12).
  3. Sc in the back loop only of each stitch around (12). This makes the sides turn up like a cup.
  4. Sc in each stitch around (12). Sl st to the next stitch and fasten off with a long tail.
  5. For the loop, chain 20 in gold (or cut 8 in of ribbon), thread both ends down through the middle of the cap and knot them inside.
  6. Sit the cap on the top of the bauble, over the closing point, and sew round its edge with the long tail.

Ideas to decorate your bauble

  • Snowy band: in a white band, stitch little Vs or crosses with red yarn to look like a Nordic jumper.
  • Christmas pudding: brown ball, a wavy white top (crochet a small circle, then sc-hdc-dc scallops round the edge) and a felt holly leaf.
  • Snowman bauble: white ball, two embroidered eyes, an orange French knot or tiny cone for the nose, and a scrap-yarn scarf (chain 30, one row of sc).
  • Granny-stripe look: change colour every 2 rounds for a candy stripe.
  • Name bauble: make the large size and embroider a short name or year across the band in backstitch (see our embroidery stitches).

How do you keep a crochet ball round?

Stuff it firmly, a little at a time, and push the stuffing outwards with the end of your hook before the hole gets small. Work the same number of plain rounds as increase rounds (the writer does this), and use the invisible decrease, which doesn't leave gaps. If it looks a bit square, roll it between your hands for a minute.

Questions crafters ask

How do you crochet an easy Christmas ornament?

Crochet a small stuffed ball: 6 sc in a magic ring, add 6 stitches a round to 30, work 5 plain rounds, then decrease 6 a round back to 6, stuffing as you go. Add a small gold cap and a chain loop to hang it.

How much yarn does a crochet bauble take?

About 10 yards for a small bauble and 15 to 25 yards for a medium or large one in worsted yarn (our estimates). A 100 g ball of worsted makes 8 to 12 baubles.

How long does it take to crochet a Christmas ornament?

About 30 to 60 minutes for a small or medium bauble once you're used to working in the round.

What yarn is best for crochet Christmas ornaments?

A smooth acrylic or cotton in worsted or DK weight. Cotton holds its shape best; add a metallic thread for a bit of sparkle.

How big is a crochet bauble?

With worsted yarn and a 3.5 mm hook, roughly 1.7 in for 24 stitches, 2.1 in for 30 and 2.5 in for 36 (our estimates). Chunky yarn makes them about half as big again.

Can I cover a plastic bauble with crochet?

Yes: work the same rounds but leave out the stuffing, slip the bauble in when the opening is about a third of the way through the decreases, then finish around it. Match the size by measuring round the bauble and comparing it with the writer's size.
