Crochet circle calculator and amigurumi ball pattern

How many stitches in each round of a flat circle in single, half double or double crochet, how many rounds for the size you want, and a full ball pattern for amigurumi, written out round by round.

Quick answer: a flat circle starts with a set number of stitches and adds that same number every round. Single crochet: 6, then +6 (6, 12, 18, 24…). Half double: 8, then +8. Double: usually 12, then +12 (some patterns use 10). Round 2 increases in every stitch, then each round puts one more plain stitch between increases. In worsted yarn, 10 rounds of single crochet makes a circle about 6 in across (our estimate).
What are you making?
Work out

Count the stitches across 4 in of single crochet. For half double or double, count across your swatch the same way: the width of each stitch is what sets the size.

Stitch counts follow the usual starting numbers (6 sc, 8 hdc, 12 dc). Sizes under each count are our estimate for worsted yarn at 12 stitches per 4 in, worked out from the distance around (stitches ÷ stitches per inch ÷ 3.14). Taller stitches make a bigger circle in fewer rounds. inc = 2 stitches in the same stitch. Even rounds start part-way into a repeat so the increases don't stack into a hexagon.

How do you crochet a flat circle?

  1. Start with a magic ring (or chain 4 and join). Work 6 sc, 8 hdc or 12 dc into it and pull the ring tight.
  2. Round 2: work 2 stitches into every stitch. You now have double the starting number.
  3. Round 3: 1 stitch, then 2 in the next stitch, all the way round.
  4. Each round after: add one more plain stitch between the increases. Round 4 has 2 plain stitches between, round 5 has 3, and so on. You add the starting number of stitches every round.
  5. Mark the first stitch of each round with a stitch marker, and count at the end of every round.

Work in a spiral for amigurumi and small pieces, or join each round with a slip stitch and chain up for coasters, rugs and mandalas. Check whether your pattern's turning chain counts as a stitch: for double crochet many patterns say chain 3 counts as the first dc, others chain 2 or 1 and don't count it.

Why is my crochet circle wavy or cupping?

Ruffles or waves mean too many stitches: you've added extra increases, or your tension is loose for the hook. Cupping or curling up means too few: an increase got missed, or you crochet tightly. Count the last round. If the number is right, try a hook one size up (for cupping) or down (for ruffles). If it's wrong, pull back to the last correct round and redo it.

Why does my circle look like a hexagon? If every increase lands on top of the one below, they line up into six corners. Start every other round part-way through a repeat (the calculator and chart do this for you), or put each increase in the second stitch of the last round's increase.

How do I crochet an amigurumi ball?

A ball is a flat circle, then straight sides, then the circle again in reverse. Work single crochet in a spiral:

  1. Increase for as many rounds as you want the ball's size (5 rounds gives 30 stitches).
  2. Work even, one sc in each stitch, for about the same number of rounds. Some designers add one more for a rounder look.
  3. Decrease the same way you increased, 6 stitches per round, until 6 are left.
  4. Stuff when the hole is still big enough (around 18 stitches), and top it up just before the last round.
  5. Fasten off, weave the tail through the front loops of the last 6 stitches, and pull it closed.

Use a hook a few sizes smaller than the yarn label asks for, so the stitches are tight and the stuffing doesn't show. An invisible decrease (through the front loops only) gives a smoother ball.

Questions crafters ask

How many stitches do you increase in a crochet circle?

Add the same number you started with, every round: 6 for single crochet, 8 for half double crochet and usually 12 for double crochet (10 in some patterns).

How many single crochet stitches in a magic ring?

Six is the usual number for a flat circle or amigurumi. Round 2 then has 12, round 3 has 18 and round 4 has 24.

How many rounds for a 6 inch crochet circle?

In worsted yarn at 12 single crochet per 4 in, about 9 rounds of single crochet, or about 5 rounds of double crochet. Your gauge decides it, so measure as you go.

Why is my crochet circle not flat?

If it ruffles, there are too many stitches; if it cups, there are too few or you crochet tightly. Count the last round, and try a different hook size if the count is right.

How do I stop my crochet circle looking like a hexagon?

Move the increases each round. Start every other round part-way into a repeat, so the increases don't sit on top of each other.

How many rounds to work even on an amigurumi ball?

About as many as you increased. Five increase rounds, five even rounds and then decrease back down to 6 stitches makes a round ball.
