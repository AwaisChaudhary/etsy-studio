Cross stitch calculator: size, fabric and floss

Enter your pattern's stitch count. See the finished size on any Aida or evenweave, how much fabric to cut, and roughly how many skeins to buy.

Quick answer: divide the stitches by the fabric count. 100 × 100 stitches on 14-count Aida is 7.1 × 7.1 in (18.1 cm). Add 3 in (7.5 cm) on every side for framing, so cut 13.1 × 13.1 in. On 28-count evenweave stitched over two, the size is the same as 14-count.

What does fabric count mean?

The count is how many stitches fit in one inch. 14-count Aida has 14 squares per inch, so it's the usual beginner's fabric: big enough to see the holes, small enough for detail. Higher counts make a smaller, finer picture from the same pattern. Evenweave and linen are counted by threads, and you usually stitch over two threads, so 28-count over two gives the same size as 14-count.

How much fabric to allow

Allow about 3 in (7.5 cm) on every side if you'll frame the piece, so there's room to stretch it and for the mat. For a hoop display, 2 in is usually enough; allow the hoop size plus about 2 in. Small finishes such as ornaments and cards need only 1 to 1.5 in.

Questions crafters ask

How do I work out the size of a cross stitch?

Divide the number of stitches by the fabric count. 140 stitches on 14-count Aida is 10 inches.

How much fabric do I need for cross stitch?

The design size plus 3 inches on every side for framing, or about 2 inches for a hoop.

How many stitches does one skein of floss make?

Roughly 1,700 to 1,800 full cross stitches on 14-count Aida with 2 strands, from one standard 8 m skein. Finer fabric makes more stitches per skein.

Is 28-count evenweave the same as 14-count Aida?

When you stitch over two threads, yes: the finished size is the same.
