How to make a pom pom (no maker needed)

A fluffy yarn pom pom takes about 10 minutes and nothing more than card, scissors and yarn. Every step is drawn below, plus the fork and finger methods, and the helper tells you what size rings to cut, how many wraps and how much yarn for the pom pom you want.

Quick answer: to make a pom pom without a maker, cut two card rings a little bigger than the pom pom you want (for a 3 in pom pom, about 3.25 in across with a 1.25 in hole), wrap yarn round them until the hole is full, cut round the outer edge between the rings, tie a strong piece of yarn tightly round the middle, remove the card and trim it round. No card? Wrap yarn round a fork (small pom poms) or two to four fingers (2 to 3 in), tie the middle, cut the loops and trim. A 3 in pom pom takes about 10 to 15 yards of worsted (our estimate).

How to make a pom pom with your fingers

Hold two fingers together for a 2 in pom pom, three or four for 2.5 to 3 in. Wrap the yarn round them 60 to 120 times (more for a fuller pom pom), not too tight. Slip a 10 in piece of yarn between your middle fingers, round the bundle, and tie it as tight as you can. Slide the loops off, double knot, cut through all the loops on both sides and trim. It's the fastest method but the least even, so trim a little more.

Which yarn makes the best pom pom?

Wool and wool blends make the fluffiest, densest pom poms because the fibres spring apart when cut. Acrylic works well and washes easily; very smooth cotton and silky yarns look stringy, and chenille sheds. Thicker yarn fills the rings faster, so chunky is great for big bobble hats. For a multi-colour pom pom, hold two or three strands together, or wrap one colour on each half of the ring.

How to attach a pom pom to a hat

Thread both long ends from the middle tie onto a tapestry needle and push them through the top of the hat, either side of the centre stitch. Tie them firmly on the inside with a double knot, then weave the ends in. For a hat you'll wash often or a removable faux-fur pom pom, sew a small button or press stud to the hat and a loop to the pom pom. For babies, sew it on very securely, or leave it off.

Pom pom problems and fixes

  • Flat or floppy: not enough wraps. Wrap until the hole is completely full, and use a smaller hole next time.
  • Falling apart: the middle tie wasn't tight enough. Use strong yarn or dental floss, pull hard and double knot.
  • Uneven: roll it between your hands, then trim the longest strands in small snips, turning it as you go. Steaming it lightly (not touching) fluffs wool.
  • Too small after trimming: cut the rings about 1/4 in bigger than the size you want; you always lose a little.

What to make with pom poms

Top a knitted beanie or crochet hat, finish a scarf or a Christmas stocking, string a garland, decorate gift wrapping, or glue tiny ones into a junk journal spread. Pom pom garlands and bobble hats also sell well at winter craft fairs: see Christmas crafts to make and sell for prices.

The methods are standard craft technique, checked against Mary Maxim, Purl Soho and Treasurie pom pom tutorials (28 Nov 2026). Questions crafters ask

How do you make a pom pom without a pom pom maker?

Cut two card rings a little bigger than the pom pom you want, wrap yarn round them until the hole is full, cut round the edge, tie the middle tightly, remove the card and trim. A fork or your fingers work for small ones.

What size cardboard circle for a pom pom?

About 1/4 in bigger across than the finished pom pom, with a hole about 40% of that width. For a 3 in pom pom, cut rings about 3.25 in across with a 1.25 in hole.

How much yarn do you need for a pom pom?

Roughly 10 to 15 yards of worsted for a 3 in pom pom and 20 to 30 yards for a 4 in one (our estimates). Thinner yarn needs more length, chunky yarn less.

How many times do you wrap yarn for a pom pom?

Until the centre hole is full. That's very roughly 150 to 200 wraps of worsted for a 3 in pom pom and 80 for a 2 in one.

What size pom pom for a hat?

About 3 to 4 in for an adult beanie, 2 to 2.5 in for a child's hat and 1.5 in for a baby hat.

How do you make a pom pom fluffy?

Use wool or a wool blend, wrap the ring really full, tie the middle very tight, then roll it in your hands and trim. A puff of steam held above the pom pom fluffs wool even more.

How do you make a pom pom with a fork?

Wrap yarn round the tines about 40 to 60 times, thread a piece of yarn through the middle gap of the fork and tie it tightly round the bundle, slide it off, cut the loops on both sides and trim.
