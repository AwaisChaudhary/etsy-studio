How to price crochet and knitting items

A fair price covers your materials, pays you for your time, and still leaves that after the shop or card reader takes its cut. Here's the formula, and a calculator that does the sums.

Quick answer: price = (materials + hours × your hourly rate) + a little for overheads, then add the selling fees. A beanie with $5 of yarn and 2.5 hours at $15 an hour is $42.50, about $47 with 10% for overheads, and about $52 on Etsy once its fees are covered. "Materials × 3" would say $15, which pays you about $4 an hour.

What is the formula for pricing crochet items?

Most experienced sellers use some version of this:

  1. Materials. Every bit that goes into or onto the item: the yarn you used (work out the share of a skein by weight), stuffing, safety eyes, buttons, labels, tags, the bag it goes in.
  2. Labour. Hours × an hourly rate. Time yourself on the next one and round up to the nearest quarter hour; most people guess low.
  3. Overheads. Tools wear out and photos, printing and trips to the post office cost money. A percentage on top covers them without tracking every penny.
  4. Fees. Whoever processes the payment takes a cut. Add it on top, or it comes out of your hourly rate.

Shipping is separate: charge what it costs you to post. (On Etsy the 6.5% transaction fee applies to the shipping you charge too.)

What hourly rate should I charge for crochet?

Start at no less than your local minimum wage. Popular crochet business guides suggest somewhere between $12 and $20 an hour in the US, and more for skilled work like garments, colourwork or custom orders. You can make your own choices about charging less for a friend, but price your shop items as a job, so a sale never costs you money.

How much are Etsy's fees on a handmade item?

  • Listing fee: $0.20 per item, renewed every 4 months or when it sells.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the order total, shipping and gift wrap included.
  • Payment processing (US): 3% + $0.25 per order.
  • Offsite Ads: 12 or 15% of an order, but only when the sale came from an ad Etsy placed for you.

Together that's roughly 9.5% plus $0.45 on most sales, which the calculator adds for you. Fees change, and they're different outside the US, so check Etsy's own fees page before you set prices for the year.

Why not just charge materials × 3?

Because it ignores time. A beanie that takes 2 hours and a blanket that takes 45 can use similar money's worth of yarn. "Materials × 3" prices them almost the same and pays you a few dollars an hour for the blanket. It works for quick, cheap-to-make items, and that's about it.

What if the price feels too high?

That's normal, and it's a sign the item takes real skill and time. You have three honest choices: sell it anyway to buyers who value handmade (craft fairs and gift buyers often do), make it faster (thicker yarn, a simpler stitch, fewer colour changes), or make something smaller that sells at a price you're happy with. Lowering your hourly rate until it hurts is the one that burns people out.

Pricing for a craft fair

  • Spread the table fee over the items you realistically expect to sell, not the number you're bringing.
  • Bring a range: a few small makes under $15 (scrunchies, coasters, keychains) so people can buy something, and bigger pieces that show what you can do.
  • Use round prices like $15, $25, $40. They're quicker to take and to make change for.

Questions crafters ask

What is the formula for pricing crochet items?

Materials + (hours × your hourly rate), plus a percentage for overheads such as tools and packaging, plus the selling fees. Shipping is charged separately.

What hourly rate should I charge for crochet?

At least your local minimum wage. Crochet business guides commonly suggest $12 to $20 an hour in the US, more for skilled or custom work.

How much should I charge for a crochet hat?

With about $5 of yarn and 2.5 hours at $15 an hour, around $45 to $50, or about $52 on Etsy once fees are covered. Quick chunky hats can be less; colourwork or fine yarn more.

How much does Etsy take from a sale?

In the US, 6.5% of the order including shipping, plus 3% + $0.25 for payment processing and $0.20 to list. That's roughly 9.5% + $0.45, more if the sale came from an Offsite Ad.

Is materials times 3 a good way to price crochet?

Only for quick items. It ignores time, so anything that takes more than an hour or two ends up paying you very little.
