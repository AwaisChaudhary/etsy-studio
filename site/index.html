Questions crafters ask

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free and works in your browser without an account. The site is supported by ads.

Will my row count be saved if I close the page?

Yes. Counters, projects and saved prompts are stored on your own device, so they're still there when you come back on the same phone or computer and browser.

Do the tools work on a phone?

Yes. Everything is built for a phone at the craft table first, with big buttons and a keep-screen-on option on the row counter.
