Fat quarter calculator: how many fat quarters do I need?

Find how many squares, strips or rectangles you can cut from one fat quarter, and how many fat quarters a whole quilt top takes. US, metric and fat eighth sizes.

Quick answer: a fat quarter is 18 x 22 in (a half yard cut in half), and you can count on about 18 x 21 in once the selvedge is trimmed. One fat quarter gives 12 five-inch squares, 9 squares of 5½ in, 56 squares of 2½ in or seven 2½ in strips. A throw quilt of 50 x 65 in made from 5 in finished squares needs about 15 fat quarters. Four fat quarters make one yard.

We add a ¼ in seam allowance all round, so a 5 in finished square is cut 5½ in.

What size is a fat quarter?

Take a half yard of quilting cotton (18 in long, the full 42 to 44 in width of the bolt) and cut it in half along the fold. Each piece is about 18 x 22 in: that's a fat quarter. A "skinny" or long quarter is a quarter yard cut the usual way, 9 x 44 in. Same amount of fabric, but the fat quarter is far more useful for squares and blocks because it's nearly square.

In the UK and Europe fabric is sold by the metre, so a fat quarter is usually 50 x 55 cm, a little bigger than the US one. A fat eighth is half a fat quarter: usually 9 x 22 in, though some shops cut 11 x 18 in, so check the listing.

How many strips from a fat quarter?

Strips from a fat quarter are about 21 in long, half the length of a strip cut across the full width of fabric. Most patterns written for jelly roll style strips (2½ in) need two fat quarter strips for every full-width strip.

Fat quarter or yardage?

  • Fat quarters are best for scrappy quilts, lots of colours, and blocks up to about 10 in. A fat quarter bundle is the easiest way to get a set of fabrics that go together.
  • Yardage is better for long strips, sashing, borders, binding and backing, which need lengths longer than 21 in.
  • The maths: 1 yard = 4 fat quarters = 8 fat eighths. If a pattern asks for ½ yard of one fabric, 2 fat quarters of it will usually do, unless it needs strips longer than 21 in.

Questions crafters ask

What size is a fat quarter?

About 18 x 22 inches: a half yard of fabric cut in half across the width. Plan on about 18 x 21 in usable once the selvedge is trimmed. A metric fat quarter is 50 x 55 cm.

How many 5 inch squares can you cut from a fat quarter?

12: three across the 18 in side and four down the 21 in side. For 5 in finished squares (cut 5½ in) you get 9.

How many 2.5 inch squares are in a fat quarter?

56: seven across and eight down an 18 x 21 in fat quarter.

How many fat quarters make a yard?

Four. A yard of 44 in wide fabric is 36 x 44 in, which cuts into four 18 x 22 in fat quarters.

How many fat quarters do I need for a throw quilt?

About 15 for a 50 x 65 in top of 5 in finished squares (130 squares at 9 per fat quarter), plus yardage for borders, binding and backing.

What is the difference between a fat quarter and a quarter yard?

Both are a quarter of a yard. A quarter yard is a long strip, 9 x 44 in; a fat quarter is a squarer 18 x 22 in, which cuts bigger squares and blocks.

What size is a fat eighth?

Usually 9 x 22 in, half a fat quarter. Some shops cut fat eighths 11 x 18 in instead.
