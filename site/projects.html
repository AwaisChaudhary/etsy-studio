Questions crafters ask

Where are my projects saved?

On this device, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Use 'Back up my projects' to download a file you can restore on another phone or computer.

What does 'frogged' mean?

Frogging is pulling a project out to reuse the yarn. It comes from 'rip it, rip it', which sounds like a frog.
