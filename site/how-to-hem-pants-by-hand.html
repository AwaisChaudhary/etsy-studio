How to hem pants by hand (invisible blind hem)

You don't need a sewing machine to shorten trousers. A hand-sewn blind hem is what tailors use on good trousers, because it hardly shows. The steps are drawn below, and the hem calculator works out exactly where to fold and cut, how much to take off and how many stitches to expect.

Quick answer: to hem pants by hand, pin the new length with your shoes on, mark a fold line, then a cut line 1.5 in below it for dress trousers (about 1 in for jeans and tapered legs). Cut, neaten the raw edge, fold up and press, then sew with a blind hem stitch: catch one or two threads of the leg, slide 1/4 to 3/8 in inside the fold, and repeat. A pair takes about 45 minutes to an hour by hand.

How to hem pants by hand, step by step

What you need

  • Needle and thread: a fine hand-sewing needle (sharps or betweens) and polyester all-purpose thread matching the trousers. On dark fabric go one shade darker; it shows less than lighter.
  • Marking: tailor's chalk or a washable fabric pen, a ruler or tape measure, and pins or clips.
  • Iron: pressing does half the work. A pressing cloth (a cotton tea towel) stops shiny marks on wool and dark fabric.
  • Sharp scissors and a seam ripper for the old hem.

How long should pants be? (the break)

The break is the little fold where the hem sits on your shoe. No break: the hem just touches the top of the shoe, a modern, cropped look for slim trousers. Slight break: one soft fold at the front; the safe choice for most suit and work trousers. Full break: a deeper fold, more traditional, for wide legs. Ankle trousers end at the ankle bone with no break at all. Whatever you pick, the back of the hem should sit about 1/2 in (1 cm) above where the heel meets the sole.

The blind hem stitch, close up

Work from right to left (left to right if you're left-handed) with the hem folded towards you. Hide the knot inside the fold. Take a tiny stitch, just one or two threads, in the trouser leg above the hem edge, then slide the needle inside the folded hem for about 1/4 to 3/8 in (6 to 10 mm) and come out. Repeat. Don't pull tight: tight stitches make dimples you can see from the outside. Every 4 in or so, make a small knot so that if the thread breaks, the whole hem won't drop. Our hand sewing stitches page draws the slip stitch and catch stitch too.

How to hem jeans by hand

Denim is thick, so a double-folded hem is easier than a blind hem. Fold the edge up 1/2 in, then 1/2 in again, press hard, and sew with a small running or back stitch close to the top fold using strong jeans thread. A thimble really helps at the side seams. If you want to keep the original hem, fold the old hem up by the amount you're taking off plus 1/4 in, back stitch right next to the old hem edge, trim the extra fabric inside and press the original hem down.

Wide leg, linen2 in (5 cm)Blind hem
Kids' trousers2 to 3 in, to let down laterLoose blind hem

On a tapered leg the trousers get narrower towards the ankle, so a deep hem is too tight to lie flat: keep it to about 1 in. See our seam allowance guide for other garments.

Questions crafters ask

How do you hem pants by hand without a sewing machine?

Pin the new length with your shoes on, mark the fold line and a cut line about 1.5 in below it, cut and neaten the edge, fold up and press, then sew all round with a blind hem stitch, catching only one or two threads of the leg each time.

How much should I leave for a hem on pants?

About 1.5 in (4 cm) for dress trousers, 1 in for tapered or slim legs, 1/2 in folded twice for jeans, and 2 in for wide-leg or linen trousers.

What is the best stitch to hem pants by hand?

The blind hem stitch (also called slip hem or invisible hem stitch). It picks up only a thread or two of the outside fabric, so almost nothing shows from the right side.

How do you hem jeans by hand and keep the original hem?

Fold the original hem up by the amount you want to take off plus 1/4 in, back stitch close to the edge of the old hem, trim the extra fabric inside and press the original hem back down.

How long does it take to hem pants by hand?

About 45 minutes to an hour for a pair, including measuring and pressing. Jeans take a little longer because the fabric is thick.

Why can I see my hem stitches from the outside?

You are taking too much of the outside fabric or pulling the thread tight. Pick up just one or two threads, keep the stitches relaxed and use thread one shade darker than the fabric.
