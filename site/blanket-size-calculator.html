Blanket size calculator

Pick a blanket size or type your own, choose your yarn, and see the finished size next to a bed, plus how many yards and skeins to buy.

Quick answer: a throw blanket is usually about 50 × 60 in (127 × 152 cm), a baby blanket 30 to 40 in square, and a queen bed blanket about 90 × 100 in. A crocheted throw in worsted yarn takes roughly 2,200–2,800 yards, and a knitted one about 1,800–2,200 yards. A crocheted baby blanket in DK takes about 1,100–1,500 yards. These are estimates for plain stitches; use the calculator for your own size and yarn.

How the yarn estimate works

We work out the blanket's area and multiply it by a typical amount of yarn per square inch for plain worsted knitting, then adjust for your yarn: thinner yarn needs more length, thicker yarn less. Crochet usually takes about a quarter more yarn than knitting, cables and bobbles take more again, and open lace stitches take a little less. Granny squares are a special case, because the joins and border add up: use the granny square blanket calculator for those.

The answer is a range, because tension, stitch and borders all change it. Buy enough for the high end, from one dye lot. For hats, scarves and other projects, use our yarn calculator, and if your swatch doesn't match the pattern, the gauge converter shows how big the blanket will really be. Knitting it? The cast on calculator tells you how many stitches to start with for your width and gauge.

Allow for the border. A few rounds of border can add 5 to 10 percent more yarn on a big blanket. Fringe or tassels add more.

What size blanket should I make?

For a gift, a throw (about 50 × 60 in) is the safest choice: it fits a sofa, the foot of a bed or a lap. For a baby, 30 to 36 inches square is easy to carry and wash. For a bed, measure the top of the mattress and add the drop you want on each side, usually 10 to 15 inches.

Making a long blanket? Keep count of rows or rounds with our row counter, and log yarn and hours in the project tracker. Not sure which yarn is which? See the yarn weight chart. Making it from squares? The granny square blanket calculator tells you how many to make.

How big is a temperature blanket?

A temperature blanket's length comes from the number of days, not a size chart: 365 rows of single crochet in worsted yarn is roughly 100 in long, longer than a queen bed. Pick the width from the chart above, then check the length, the colours and the yarn for each colour with our temperature blanket planner.

How do I make a blanket bigger or smaller?

For a blanket worked in rows, change the width with the foundation chain or cast-on (the cast on calculator or, for crochet, the foundation chain calculator does the maths), and the length by working more or fewer rows. Going up one size, say from throw to twin, roughly doubles the yarn, so check the new amount above before you start. Working corner to corner? Questions crafters ask

How many yards of yarn for a baby blanket?

A crocheted 36 × 36 in baby blanket takes roughly 1,100–1,500 yards of DK yarn or 1,000–1,200 yards of worsted. A knitted one takes a little less. These are estimates for plain stitches.

How long is a temperature blanket?

One row a day for a year is 365 rows, which is roughly 100 in in worsted single crochet at a typical gauge. Thinner yarn, garter stitch or a square per day keeps it shorter. Our temperature blanket planner works it out for your stitch and gauge.

What size is a crochet throw blanket?

Most throws are about 50 × 60 inches (127 × 152 cm). Some patterns go up to 54 × 72 inches for a longer sofa blanket.

What size is a baby blanket?

A baby or receiving blanket is usually 30 to 40 inches square. A stroller blanket is a little smaller, about 30 × 36 inches, and a crib blanket can be as big as 45 × 60 inches.

How much yarn for a queen size crochet blanket?

Very roughly 6,700 to 8,500 yards of worsted weight yarn for plain crochet, which is often 34 to 43 skeins of 200 yards. Chunky yarn needs far fewer yards. Use the calculator for your own yarn, and treat it as an estimate.

How many skeins for a throw blanket?

In worsted yarn, a knitted throw takes roughly 1,800 to 2,200 yards and a crocheted one about 2,300 to 2,800 yards, so roughly 10 to 15 skeins of 200 yards once you allow a little spare. These are estimates.
