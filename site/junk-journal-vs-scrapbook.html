Junk journal vs scrapbook: what's the difference?

A scrapbook tells a story with photos on neat, photo-safe album pages. A junk journal is a handmade book of found paper, collage and writing. Here's how they differ, a 5-question quiz to see which suits you, and how to combine them.

Quick answer: a scrapbook is built around photos: planned layouts on acid-free pages in an album, made to preserve memories for years. A junk journal is a handmade book of recycled and found paper (book pages, envelopes, tickets, tea-stained paper) for collage, keepsakes and writing, with a layered, vintage look. Scrapbooks focus on the story in the photos; junk journals on the making, the textures and your own words. Many people do both.

What is a scrapbook?

A scrapbook is an album of decorated pages that tell the story of something: a holiday, a baby's first year, a wedding. The photos are the stars. Pages are usually 12 x 12 in or 8.5 x 11 in in clear page protectors, and the papers, stickers and adhesives are labelled acid-free and lignin-free so the photos don't yellow. Layouts tend to be planned: a background paper, photos on mats, a title and a short caption. See every album size and how many photos fit with our scrapbook page size calculator.

What is a junk journal?

A junk journal is a book you make yourself from paper that would otherwise be thrown away: old book pages, envelopes, receipts, packaging, maps, sewing patterns and wrapping paper, often stained with tea or coffee. Pages are folded into signatures and sewn into a cover. Inside you add pockets, tags, flip-outs and bits of ephemera, and use it as a diary, an art journal or a memory book. Start with our junk journaling for beginners guide and the supply checklist.

The main differences

  • Purpose: scrapbooks document events; junk journals are as much about the making and writing as the memory.
  • Materials: scrapbooks use bought papers made for the job; junk journals use found and recycled paper.
  • Structure: scrapbook pages sit in an album and can be moved; a junk journal is a bound book.
  • Longevity: scrapbook products are made to protect photos; junk journal paper often isn't archival.
  • Style: clean and planned versus layered, textured and imperfect.

Can you use a junk journal as a scrapbook?

Yes, and lots of people do: a travel junk journal with tickets, maps and photos from the trip is both. To keep photos safe, use prints or copies rather than one-off originals, slip them into tuck spots or photo corners instead of gluing them flat, and keep acidic paper (old book pages, newspaper) from touching the photo face. The signature calculator helps you plan a thicker book with room for bulky pages.

Supplies you can share between the two

  • Patterned paper, cardstock and journaling cards
  • A paper trimmer, scissors, a bone folder and a corner rounder
  • Glue sticks and double-sided tape (acid-free for anything near photos)
  • Washi tape, stamps, stickers and die cuts
  • Ephemera: see 62 junk journal ephemera ideas

Which is cheaper?

Junk journaling is cheaper to start: a needle, thread, glue and your recycling bin are enough for a first book, and our tea staining guide turns plain paper into vintage pages for pennies. Scrapbooking needs an album, protectors and photo-safe papers, but those last for many projects. Either way, printable kits save time: print a sheet of tags or cards whenever you need them.

Questions crafters ask

What is the difference between a junk journal and a scrapbook?

A scrapbook is a photo-focused album with planned layouts on acid-free pages. A junk journal is a handmade book of found and recycled paper used for collage, keepsakes and writing, with a layered, vintage look.

Is junk journaling the same as scrapbooking?

No, though they overlap. Both use paper and memories, but scrapbooking is built around photos and preservation, while junk journaling is about making a book from scraps and filling it however you like.

Can I put photos in a junk journal?

Yes. Use prints or copies, tuck them into pockets or photo corners, and keep acidic paper like old book pages from touching the photo face so they don't yellow.

Which is better for beginners, junk journal or scrapbook?

Junk journaling costs less to start because much of the paper is free. Scrapbooking is easier if you want quick, neat pages around your photos. Try the quiz above to see which suits you.

Is a junk journal archival?

Usually not. Recycled paper, book pages and tea-stained paper are often acidic and can yellow over time. Scrapbook products are labelled acid-free and lignin-free for long life.

What size is a junk journal?

Any size. Popular sizes are about 5.5 x 8.5 in (half letter) and A5, because a letter or A4 sheet folds in half to make the pages.
