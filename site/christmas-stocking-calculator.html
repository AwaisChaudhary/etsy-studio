Christmas stocking size and yarn calculator

Pick a stocking size, your yarn and knitting or crochet: see how much yarn to buy, how many stitches to start with, and whether a name fits on the cuff.

Quick answer: a standard Christmas stocking is about 18 to 20 in (46–51 cm) tall from the top of the cuff to the heel and 7 to 8 in wide; minis are about 7 in and "extra long" shop stockings 26 in. By our estimate a standard stocking takes roughly 210–290 yd of worsted to knit, 270–370 yd to crochet, or 150–210 yd of bulky yarn, folded cuff included. Knit it in the round on about 64 stitches in worsted.

How many stitches to cast on for a stocking

A stocking knit in the round is a tube twice the flat width around. Multiply that by your stitches per inch and round to a multiple of 4, so the heel can use exactly half the stitches. Our numbers, at the Craft Yarn Council's middle gauge for each weight:

Knitting it flat in two pieces instead? Cast on half the number plus 2 for the seam on each piece, or follow our step-by-step how to knit a Christmas stocking (flat, no heel turn, with a full pattern writer). Your own gauge matters more than the label, so knit a small swatch and use the cast on calculator or the gauge converter if your numbers differ. Crocheting a stocking

Crochet stockings are usually made as two flat panels joined around the edge, or worked in rounds from the cuff down. For two panels, chain the flat width at your gauge plus 1. For rounds, chain twice the flat width and join, then work single crochet or half double crochet in rounds. Crochet uses about a quarter more yarn than knitting for the same size, which the calculator allows for. New to the stitches? Start with basic crochet stitches, and use the foundation chain calculator for the starting chain.

Which yarn is best for a Christmas stocking?

  • Worsted or aran acrylic is the classic: cheap, washable, holds its shape when it's full of gifts.
  • Bulky or chunky is the fastest. A standard stocking in bulky is a weekend make for most knitters and crocheters.
  • Wool gives the crisp stitches you want for Fair Isle names and motifs, and it doesn't stretch as much when filled.
  • Furry or eyelash yarn makes a Santa-style cuff. It hides stitches, so use it only for the cuff and allow extra yarn.

Putting a name on the cuff

The easiest way to add a name is duplicate stitch: knit the cuff plain, then embroider the letters over the stitches with a needle afterwards. You can plan it on squared paper or with the cross stitch calculator's grid thinking. A simple blocky alphabet is about 5 stitches wide and 7 rows tall, plus a 1-stitch gap between letters. The calculator tells you how many letters fit across the front. Long name? Use a bigger stocking, a finer yarn, or initials. In crochet, tapestry crochet or surface slip stitch works the same way; embroidery stitches like backstitch and chain stitch work on felt cuffs too.

Will it be ready by Christmas?

A standard worsted stocking is roughly 8 to 14 hours of knitting for most people, less in bulky or for a mini. Questions crafters ask

What size is a standard Christmas stocking?

Most are about 18 to 20 inches (46 to 51 cm) from the top of the cuff to the heel and 7 to 8 inches wide. Minis for trees and advent calendars are about 7 inches, and extra long stockings about 26 inches.

How much yarn do I need to knit a Christmas stocking?

By our estimate a standard 18 inch stocking takes about 210–290 yards of worsted yarn, or 150–210 yards of bulky, with a folded cuff. Plan the cuff, heel and toe colours separately and buy a little extra.

How much yarn do I need to crochet a Christmas stocking?

About a quarter more than knitting: roughly 270–370 yards of worsted for a standard 18 inch stocking, by our estimate.

How many stitches do I cast on for a Christmas stocking?

Twice the flat width times your stitches per inch, rounded to a multiple of 4. For a 7 inch wide stocking in worsted at 18 stitches per 4 inches that's 64 stitches in the round.

How long does it take to make a Christmas stocking?

A standard worsted stocking takes most people roughly 8 to 14 hours to knit or crochet, a bulky one less, and a mini 1 to 2 hours. Colourwork names add time.

How do I add a name to a knitted stocking?

Knit the cuff plain and add the name afterwards in duplicate stitch, embroidering over the stitches. Each letter is about 5 stitches wide and 7 rows tall plus a 1 stitch gap; the calculator shows how many letters fit.
