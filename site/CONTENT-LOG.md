Linked from footer Tools, home guides band, blanket size calculator and crochet terms.
- Targets: "how many granny squares for a blanket", "granny square blanket calculator", "how many 6 inch granny squares for a queen blanket", "how much yarn for a granny square blanket", "how many granny squares for a baby blanket".
- Sources: square counts method and seam/border allowance cross-checked with Darn Good Yarn's granny square guide (6 in squares, twin about 150 squares); yarn per 5 in worsted square 25-30 yd from Marching North's classic granny square; whole-blanket check from B.Hooked's classic granny blanket (50 × 66 in throw, about 3,330 yd worsted) and Mary Maxim's granny throw. All labelled estimates.
