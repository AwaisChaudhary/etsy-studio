Free crochet headband pattern (easy ear warmer)

This ribbed ear warmer is a great first wearable and a quick Christmas gift: it's one stretchy strip of half double crochet with a twist at the front. Pick a size, yarn and width and the pattern below works out your chain, rows and yarn.

Quick answer: to crochet an easy ear warmer, chain the width (about 12 chains for a 3.5 in adult headband in worsted), work half double crochet in the back loop only, row after row, until the strip stretches snugly round the head (about 20 in for an average adult head of 22 in), then fold the ends and sew them together for a twist. It takes about 1.5 to 2.5 hours and roughly 60 to 80 yards of worsted yarn (our estimates); chunky yarn is quicker.

How to crochet an ear warmer, step by step

What you need

  • Yarn: about 60 to 80 yards of worsted, or 50 to 70 of chunky, for an adult (the writer works out yours). A soft acrylic, wool blend or anything with a little stretch is ideal; 100% cotton has no bounce and will feel loose.
  • Hook: the size on the label: about 5.5 mm (I-9) for worsted, 8 mm (L-11) for chunky, 10 mm (N/P-15) for super bulky.
  • Also: scissors, a tapestry needle and a couple of clips or pins for the twist.

Why work in the back loop?

Every crochet stitch has two loops on top. Working only into the back one leaves the front loop as a ridge, and row after row those ridges stack up like knitted ribbing. The fabric stretches far more lengthwise, which is exactly what a headband needs: it hugs the head instead of slipping. That's why the strip is made a little shorter than the head (about 90%). New to the stitch? See basic crochet stitches for the half double crochet.

What size should a crochet headband be?

Measure round the forehead and over the tops of the ears. Typical heads are about 17 in for a baby, 18.5 in for a toddler, 20 in for a child, 21 in for a teen and 22 to 23.5 in for adults (our hat size chart has the full table from the Craft Yarn Council). Make a ribbed band 1.5 to 2.5 in shorter than the head; less for a non-stretchy stitch. Widths of 2.5 to 3 in suit children and 3.5 to 4.5 in suit adults; wider bands keep ears warmer.

Ear warmer problems and fixes

  • It's too loose: take off a few rows before you seam. Ribbing relaxes with wear, so snug is right.
  • The edges wave: you're adding or skipping a stitch at the ends. Count the stitches on every row for the first few, and remember the chain 2 doesn't count as a stitch.
  • The twist looks bulky: use a slimmer width or a lighter yarn, or choose the plain seam and add a button or a little pom pom.
  • The seam shows: sew with the yarn tail through the back loops only, then turn it so the seam sits at the back of the head.

Questions crafters ask

How do you crochet an easy ear warmer?

Chain the width (about 12 for an adult in worsted), work half double crochet in the back loop only, row after row, until the strip stretches snugly round the head, then fold each end in half, hook them together and sew for a twisted front.

How long should a crochet headband be?

About 1.5 to 2.5 in shorter than the head when it's ribbed, because it stretches. For an average adult head of 22 in, make the strip about 20 in long.

How much yarn do you need for a crochet ear warmer?

Roughly 60 to 80 yards of worsted or 50 to 70 yards of chunky yarn for an adult, by our estimates. One skein makes two or three.

How wide should an ear warmer be?

3.5 to 4.5 in for adults and 2.5 to 3 in for children. Wider bands cover the ears better.

How long does it take to crochet an ear warmer?

About 1.5 to 2.5 hours in worsted yarn and 1 to 1.5 hours in chunky for most crocheters, by our estimates.

What is the best yarn for a crochet headband?

A soft acrylic, wool or wool blend in worsted or chunky. It needs some stretch, so avoid 100% cotton.
