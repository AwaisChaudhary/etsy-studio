Free 2027 calendar printable

Make your own 2027 calendar: one page for the whole year, or a page a month, with US or UK holidays, Sunday or Monday start, on US Letter or A4. Print it or save it.

Quick answer: 2027 starts on a Friday (1 January) and ends on a Friday (31 December). It is not a leap year, so it has 365 days and 52 ISO weeks. Easter Sunday is 28 March 2027, Thanksgiving is Thursday 25 November, and Christmas Day falls on a Saturday. A 2027 calendar has the same layout as 2021, so a 2021 wall calendar lines up day for day.

When a federal holiday falls on a Saturday it's usually observed on the Friday before, and on a Sunday the Monday after (the US Office of Personnel Management rule). That's why New Year's Day 2028, a Saturday, is observed on Friday 31 December 2027. Other days people plan around: Valentine's Day (Sun 14 Feb), Mother's Day (Sun 9 May), Father's Day (Sun 20 Jun) and Halloween (Sun 31 Oct).

Which 2027 calendar layout should I print?

  • Whole year on one page: for the fridge, a planner cover, or counting weeks to a deadline. Holidays are listed along the bottom.
  • A month per page: big boxes to write appointments in. Pin it up or punch it for a binder.
  • Monthly planner: the month grid plus a "Projects this month" checklist, a supplies list and notes. Made for makers: plan which blanket rows, gifts or market stock you'll finish each month.

Sunday or Monday start?

US calendars usually start the week on Sunday; UK, European and ISO week calendars start on Monday. Pick whichever matches your diary so the days line up.

Questions crafters ask

What day does 2027 start on?

Friday 1 January 2027. It also ends on a Friday, 31 December 2027.

Is 2027 a leap year?

No. 2027 has 365 days. The next leap year is 2028, which has 29 February.

Which year has the same calendar as 2027?

2021 and 2010. Both started on a Friday and weren't leap years, so every date falls on the same weekday. The next repeat is 2038.

When is Easter 2027?

Sunday 28 March 2027. Good Friday is 26 March and Easter Monday is 29 March.

How many weeks are in 2027?

52 ISO weeks. Week 1 starts on Monday 4 January 2027, and week 52 ends on Sunday 2 January 2028.

Is this 2027 calendar printable free?

Yes. Print as many as you like, on US Letter or A4, or save them as a PDF or SVG image. Nothing to sign up for.
