Free crochet basket pattern (any size, plus a yarn bowl)

A round crochet basket is just three parts: a flat circle, one round that turns the corner, and straight walls. This free beginner pattern writes every round for any size, from a little yarn bowl to a big storage basket, with handle slots, a yarn hole and an estimate of how much yarn to buy.

Quick answer: to crochet a basket, work a flat circle of single crochet (6 sc in a magic ring, then 6 increases every round) until it is as wide as you want the base. Work one round in the back loops only to turn the corner, then sc every round with no increases for the walls. Use thick yarn (or two strands held together) and a hook one or two sizes smaller than the label says, so the basket is firm. A 6 in yarn bowl takes roughly 120 to 200 yards of worsted held double, by our estimates.

How to crochet a basket, step by step

What you need

  • Yarn: something thick and not too stretchy. Super bulky (6) yarn, T-shirt yarn or cotton macrame cord makes a basket that stands up on its own. Worsted (4) works too if you hold two strands together. Cotton holds its shape better than acrylic.
  • Hook: one or two sizes smaller than the yarn label suggests, so the stitches are tight: about 6 mm (US J) for worsted held double, 8 to 9 mm (US L to M) for super bulky, 10 to 12 mm for T-shirt yarn.
  • Also: a stitch marker (a scrap of yarn works), scissors and a large-eye tapestry needle.

New to crochet? Learn the magic ring and single crochet first; everything else is explained here.

How to make the base lie flat

A single crochet circle lies flat when you add 6 stitches every round. If the edge starts to ripple like a frill, you are adding too many (or your stitches are loose); if it cups up like a bowl, you are adding too few. Count at the end of every round: round 5 should have 30 stitches, round 10 should have 60. If you stack increases in the same place each round, the circle turns into a hexagon, so the writer shifts the increase by half a repeat on every other round. Our crochet circle calculator does the same maths for hdc and dc circles.

How to make a crochet basket stiff

  • Tighter stitches: a smaller hook is the biggest help. The fabric should feel almost hard to push the hook through.
  • More yarn: hold two or three strands together, or use cord or T-shirt yarn.
  • Waistcoat stitch (sc worked between the legs of the stitch below) is denser than plain sc and makes very firm walls.
  • Fold-down cuff: work the walls 2 in taller and fold the top over. The double layer stands up well.
  • Stiffener: for a cotton yarn bowl, soak it in fabric stiffener or a 1:1 mix of PVA glue and water, shape it over a bowl and let it dry (see our stiffening guide).

Crochet yarn bowl: how it works

A yarn bowl stops your ball rolling away while you crochet or knit. Pick the yarn hole option and the writer puts a small hole near the top of the wall: thread your working yarn through it from the inside, drop the ball in, and pull as you work. A crocheted bowl is soft, light and won't break if it falls; a ceramic or wooden one is heavier and holds the ball steadier. A 6 in wide, 4 in tall bowl fits most 100 g balls. To wind a centre-pull ball first, the ball will hardly move at all.

Square and oval baskets

For an oval basket, start with a chain as long as the difference between the length and the width, and work round both sides of it, increasing at each end. For a square one, start with a granny-style square or work 4 corners of 3 sc. Both use the same back-loop round and straight walls. This page writes round baskets, which are the easiest to get right first time.

Selling crochet baskets

Baskets sell well at craft fairs and online as plant pot covers, nursery storage and yarn bowls. Price for materials and your time, not just the yarn: our crochet pricing guide and craft fair profit calculator help.

Questions crafters ask

How do you crochet a basket for beginners?

Crochet a flat circle in single crochet, adding 6 stitches every round, until it is the width of the base. Work one round in the back loops only to turn the corner, then single crochet every round without increasing until the walls are tall enough. Finish with a slip stitch or crab stitch edge.

What yarn is best for a crochet basket?

Thick, firm yarn: super bulky (6) yarn, T-shirt yarn or cotton cord. Worsted yarn works if you hold two strands together. Use a hook one or two sizes smaller than the label suggests so the basket is stiff.

How do I make my crochet basket stand up?

Use a smaller hook, thicker yarn or two strands together, and make the turning round in the back loops only. A fold-down cuff, waistcoat stitch or fabric stiffener also help.

How much yarn do I need for a crochet basket?

A 7 in wide, 6 in tall basket takes roughly 100 to 150 yards of super bulky yarn; a large storage basket can take 300 yards or more. The pattern writer above estimates it for your size and yarn.

How do you make a crochet yarn bowl?

Make a small round basket about 6 in wide and 4 in tall, and leave a hole near the top by chaining 3 and skipping 3 stitches on one round. Thread your working yarn through the hole from the inside.

Why is my crochet basket base curling?

If it cups upward you are adding too few stitches or crocheting tightly; if it ripples, too many. Add exactly 6 stitches every round and count at the end of each round.
