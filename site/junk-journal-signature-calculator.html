Junk journal signature calculator

Pick your paper, how many sheets go in each signature and how many signatures you want. Get the page count, finished page size, how thick the spine will be, where to punch the sewing holes and how much thread to cut.

Quick answer: every sheet you fold in half makes 2 leaves and 4 pages. A signature of 5 sheets has 20 pages, and a journal of 4 such signatures has 80 pages. US Letter paper folded in half gives 5.5 × 8.5 in pages; A4 gives A5 pages. Most junk journalers use 4 to 8 sheets per signature, sewn with 3 or 5 holes and about 3 times the spine height of thread.

What is a signature in a junk journal?

A signature is a small stack of sheets folded in half together, so they nest inside each other and share one fold. You sew through the fold to hold them together. One signature makes a slim booklet; sew several into one cover and you have a thicker journal. Printers use the same word for the folded sections of a book.

How many sheets should go in a signature?

Most junk journals use 4 to 8 sheets per signature. A Coptic-bound travel journal tutorial we read uses 4 folded sheets per signature (16 pages); our beginner's guide suggests 5 to 8 sheets for a first project. Thin paper can take more; cardstock or thick found papers fewer. Too many sheets and the fold won't close flat, and the inner sheets stick out at the edge (trim them, or keep the uneven look).

How many signatures does a junk journal need?

There's no fixed number. A slim travel journal might have 1 or 2, a chunky "stuffed" journal 4 to 6. Remember that a junk journal grows: every pocket, tag and tuck-in adds thickness, so leave room in the spine. The calculator's ephemera setting adds that bulk to the spine estimate.

How do I sew a signature with a pamphlet stitch?

  1. Mark the holes on a strip of scrap card folded like your signature: one in the centre of the fold and the rest evenly spaced, with the end holes about ½ to ¾ in from the top and bottom.
  2. Pierce the fold from the inside using the strip as a guide, with an awl or a thick needle. An old phone book or folded towel underneath helps.
  3. Cut the thread about 3 times the height of the spine.
  4. For 3 holes: go out through the centre from inside (leave a tail), in at the top hole, out at the bottom hole, then back in through the centre. Tie the two ends around the long stitch and trim.
  5. For 5 holes: work the same way, filling in the extra holes in a figure-of-eight and finishing back in the centre.
Sewing into a cover? Punch matching holes in the cover's spine, one set for each signature, spaced a little apart so the signatures can sit side by side. Each signature then gets its own pamphlet stitch through the cover.

How thick will my junk journal be?

Each folded sheet adds two layers of paper at the spine. Printer paper is about 0.1 mm thick, so 4 signatures of 5 sheets come to roughly 4 to 5 mm before anything is added. Pockets, tags and ephemera can double that. The calculator gives an estimate with a range; measure a stack of your own paper for a closer number, and make the cover spine a little wider than you think.

Which paper should I use for signatures?

Printer paper folds cleanly and is easy to sew. Mix in old book pages, envelopes, kraft paper, graph paper and tea-stained pages for the layered look. If a fold cracks or fights you, try folding the sheet the other way: paper folds more easily along its grain.

Questions crafters ask

How many pages are in a signature?

Each folded sheet makes 4 pages (2 leaves). A signature of 4 sheets has 16 pages, 5 sheets 20 pages and 8 sheets 32 pages.

How many sheets of paper should be in a junk journal signature?

Most junk journals use 4 to 8 folded sheets per signature. Use fewer with thick paper or cardstock so the fold still closes flat.

How many signatures should a junk journal have?

There is no set number: 1 or 2 for a slim travel journal, 4 to 6 for a chunky one. Leave room in the spine for the pockets and ephemera you'll add.

What size is a junk journal made from letter paper?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) folded in half gives 5.5 × 8.5 in pages, the most common junk journal size. A4 folded in half gives A5 pages (about 5.8 × 8.3 in).

How much thread do I need to sew a signature?

For a pamphlet stitch, cut about 3 times the height of the spine. For an 8.5 in tall journal that's about 26 in (0.7 yd) per signature.

How many holes do I need to sew a signature?

Use an odd number so you start and finish in the centre: 3 holes for small journals and 5 for most letter-size journals. Very tall journals can use 7.
