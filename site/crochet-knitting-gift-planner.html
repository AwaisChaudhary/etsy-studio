    How the planner works

    It takes your hours per week and the days left until your finish date to work out how much crafting time you really have. Then it lines your gifts up in the order you listed them, like beads on a strand, and shows roughly when each one will be done. Anything that runs past the deadline turns red.

    Fine yarn makes small stitches, so it takes longer to cover the same area; chunky yarn works up much faster. The planner allows about 30 percent more time for fine yarn and about a third less for chunky. If you've timed yourself on a similar project, type your own hours instead.

    Time yourself once. Work for 30 minutes on your swatch or the first rows of the gift and count how much you finished. Multiply out to the whole piece and you'll have a far better number than any chart.

    Making it all fit

    • Start the biggest gift first. Put blankets and sweaters at the top of the list so the small things fill the last weeks.
    • Swap the yarn, not the gift. A hat in chunky yarn can take half the time of one in DK.
    • Keep a few quick makes in reserve. Dishcloths, ornaments and headbands take an evening or two.
    • Give an IOU. A wrapped skein with a note saying what it will become is a lovely gift too.

    Once you know what you're making, check how much yarn to buy with the yarn calculator or, for blankets, the blanket size calculator. Keep notes on each gift in the project tracker, and count rows with the row counter.

    Questions crafters ask

    How long does it take to knit a hat?

    Roughly 7 to 11 hours for a simple adult hat in worsted yarn, depending on experience. A chunky hat can be done in an evening or two.

    How long does it take to crochet a blanket?

    A throw often takes 20 to 30 hours of crochet, and a knitted one about twice as long. Bigger blankets, fine yarn and fancy stitches take longer.

    What can I knit or crochet in one evening?

    A dishcloth, an ornament, a headband or a chunky cowl are all realistic in one to three hours for most makers.

    Is it too late to make Christmas gifts?

    Usually not. Even one hour a day gives you over 30 hours in five weeks, enough for a couple of hats and a scarf. Put your numbers into the planner to see what fits.
