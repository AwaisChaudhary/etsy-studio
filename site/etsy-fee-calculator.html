Etsy fee calculator for handmade sellers

Enter your price, shipping and costs. See every Etsy fee, what you keep, and the price you need to charge for the profit you want.

Quick answer: on a US sale Etsy takes a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing. That is about 9.5% + $0.45 per order, or about a quarter of the order if the sale came through an Offsite Ad (15%, or 12% for shops over $10,000 a year).

Etsy Offsite Ads fee: how it works

Etsy advertises some listings on Google, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest and other sites. If a buyer clicks one of those ads and buys from your shop within 30 days, you pay an Offsite Ads fee on that order:

  • 15% if your shop has sold under $10,000 in every 365-day period. You're enrolled automatically, but you can opt out in Shop Manager, Settings, Offsite Ads.
  • 12% once your shop has sold $10,000 or more in any 365 days. From then on you can't opt out.
  • The fee for one order is capped at $100 (USD), however big the order. The calculator above applies the cap.
  • No sale, no fee: clicks that don't lead to an order cost nothing. You can't set a budget or pick which listings Etsy advertises.

Should a small shop opt out? Work out an ad sale in the calculator. If a 15% ad sale still leaves you a profit you're happy with, the extra buyers can be worth it; if it wipes out your margin on low-priced items, raise those prices or opt out. Check the "Offsite Ads" line in your monthly Etsy statement to see how many sales really came from ads.

Offsite Ads rules from Etsy's help article "How Etsy's Offsite Ads Work", checked 27 November 2026.

Three ways handmade sellers lose money on fees

  • Free shipping without raising the price. The 6.5% applies to shipping too, so moving postage into the item price costs the same in fees but the postage still has to be paid.
  • Cheap items. The fixed $0.45 (listing + processing) eats a much bigger share of a $5 bookmark than of a $60 blanket. Bundle small makes, or price them in sets.
  • Forgetting Offsite Ads. One ad sale can take 12 to 15% more. Price so that even an ad sale still pays for your materials.

Questions crafters ask

How much does Etsy take per sale?

In the US, $0.20 to list, 6.5% of the item price plus shipping, and 3% + $0.25 for payment processing. That is about 9.5% + $0.45 per order. If the sale came from an Offsite Ad, add 15% (12% for shops selling over $10,000 a year).

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee and the payment processing fee both apply to the shipping you charge, not just the item price.

What are Etsy fees in the UK?

The same $0.20 listing fee and 6.5% transaction fee, with payment processing of 4% + £0.20. UK shops can also be charged VAT on Etsy's fees.

How do I price my item to cover Etsy fees?

Add your materials, postage, the fixed fees and the profit you want, then divide by what is left after the percentage fees (about 0.905 in the US). The calculator above does this for you.

Can I turn off Etsy Offsite Ads?

Shops that have never sold $10,000 or more in a 365-day period can opt out in Shop Manager, Settings, Offsite Ads. Once a shop passes $10,000, it's enrolled at the 12% rate for good.

Is the Etsy Offsite Ads fee capped?

Yes. Etsy says the Offsite Ads fee on any one order never goes above $100 USD, whatever the order total.

How long after an ad click does Etsy charge the Offsite Ads fee?

30 days. If a buyer clicks an Offsite Ad and buys from your shop within 30 days, that order carries the 15% or 12% fee.
