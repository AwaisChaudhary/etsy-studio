How to crochet a hat for beginners

A simple beanie is one of the best first crochet projects: you start with a flat circle for the top, then work round and round without increasing until it's long enough. Every step is drawn below, and the writer gives you every round for your size and yarn, in US or UK terms.

Quick answer: to crochet a beanie from the top down, make a magic ring and work 12 double crochet into it, then add 12 stitches every round (2 dc in each stitch, then 1 dc and 2 dc, then 2 dc and 2 dc…) until the flat circle is about 6.2 in (16 cm) across for an average adult. Then work 1 dc in each stitch until the hat is about 8 in (20 cm) from the centre top, and finish with 2 rounds of sc for the brim. In worsted yarn with a 5.5 to 6 mm hook that's about 5 increase rounds (around 54 to 60 stitches, depending on your tension) and roughly 170 to 220 yd of yarn (our estimate). The writer below does the maths for any size, yarn and stitch.

How to crochet a beanie, step by step

What you need for your first hat

  • Yarn: a smooth worsted (weight 4) in a light colour so you can see the stitches. Acrylic or a wool blend is soft, warm and easy to wash. An adult beanie takes about 170 to 220 yards; a baby hat 70 to 120 (our estimates, the writer gives yours).
  • Hook: one or two sizes bigger than the label is normal for a soft, drapey hat, for example 5.5 or 6 mm (I-9 or J-10) for worsted.
  • Also: a stitch marker for the first stitch of each round, a tape measure, scissors and a tapestry needle. Card or a pom pom maker if you want a pom pom.

How many stitches to start a crochet hat?

Top-down hats start with a set number in the ring and add that many every round, because that's what keeps a circle flat: 6 for single crochet, 8 (or 10) for half double, 12 for double crochet. Taller stitches need more because each round is taller and the circle grows faster. If your circle ruffles, you're adding too many (or your hook is too big); if it cups into a bowl, too few. Our crochet circle calculator writes a flat circle for any start number.

How big should the crown of a hat be?

Divide the hat's width around by 3.14. A finished hat is usually about 10 to 12% smaller around than the head so it stays on, so an average 22 in adult head wants a hat of about 19.5 in, and a flat top of about 6.2 in (16 cm) across. Measure across the middle of the circle, not round the edge. The full table, preemie to large adult, is on our hat size chart.

How long should a crochet beanie be?

Measure from the centre of the top down to the edge. (The taller hat heights on our size chart are the Craft Yarn Council's full-coverage figures, which suit a folded brim.) A fitted adult beanie is about 8 to 8.5 in (20 to 22 cm); add 1.5 to 2 in for a folded brim and 2 to 3 in for a slouchy hat. Children's hats are 6 to 7.5 in and baby hats 5 to 6.5 in. Try it on as you go: crochet stretches a little lengthways when it's worn.

Crochet hat problems and fixes

  • Seam slants or wanders: that's normal in joined rounds. Keep it at the back, or turn after each round to keep it straighter.
  • Too tight: go up a hook size, or add one more increase round before working even.
  • Too loose or wavy brim: work the brim with a hook one size smaller, or sc2tog every 10th stitch in the first brim round.
  • Lost count: count stitches at the end of every increase round; they should always be a multiple of your start number. A row counter helps.

Ribbed brim with post stitches

For a stretchy, knit-look brim, alternate front post double crochet (FPdc) and back post double crochet (BPdc): work the stitch round the post of the stitch below, from the front or from the back. On an even number of stitches, repeat (FPdc, BPdc) all the way round, then line up the same stitches on the next rounds. Working in the round only in the back loop of single crochet also gives a soft ridged edge. Need more stitch help? Gifting or selling hats

A beanie takes 2 to 4 hours in worsted double crochet (more in single crochet), so they're ideal last-minute Christmas gifts (even quicker: a ribbed crochet ear warmer): check timings in the handmade gift planner. Questions crafters ask

How do you crochet a hat for beginners?

Start with a magic ring, work 12 double crochet into it and add 12 stitches every round until the flat circle is the right width across (6 to 6.5 in for an adult). Then work 1 stitch in each stitch until the hat is about 8 in long, and finish with a brim.

How many stitches do you start a crochet hat with?

Usually 12 for double crochet, 8 or 10 for half double crochet and 6 for single crochet, worked into a magic ring. You then add the same number every round.

How wide should the top of a crochet hat be?

The hat's width around divided by 3.14. For an average adult that's about 6.2 in (16 cm) across; for a toddler about 5.3 in.

How long should a crochet beanie be?

About 8 to 8.5 in from the top for a fitted adult beanie, 10 to 11 in for a slouchy one, 6 to 7.5 in for a child and 5 to 6.5 in for a baby.

How much yarn do I need to crochet a hat?

Roughly 170 to 220 yards of worsted for an adult double crochet beanie and 70 to 120 yards for a baby hat (our estimates). Single crochet uses more than double crochet.

How long does it take to crochet a hat?

About 2 to 4 hours for an adult beanie in worsted double crochet once you know the stitches; single crochet and thinner yarn take longer.

What size hook for a crochet hat?

With worsted yarn, a 5 to 6 mm hook (H-8 to J-10) is typical. Go up a size for a softer, slouchier hat or down for a firmer one.
