How to knit a hat for beginners

A plain beanie knit in the round is a great second project after a scarf: a ribbed brim, smooth knit rounds and a neat crown. Every step is drawn below, and the writer gives you the cast-on, the brim, the length and every crown round for your size and yarn.

Quick answer: to knit a beginner beanie, cast on 88 stitches in worsted yarn on a 16 in, 5 mm (US 8) circular needle for an average adult, join in the round, work k2, p2 rib for 2 in, then knit every round until the hat is about 6 in from the cast-on edge. Shape the crown with 8 decreases every other round (k9, k2tog; then k8, k2tog…), switching to every round near the top, until 8 stitches are left. Thread the yarn through them and pull tight. That's roughly 150 to 190 yd of worsted (our estimate). The writer below does it for any size and yarn.

How to knit a beanie, step by step

What you need

  • Yarn: a smooth worsted (weight 4) wool or wool blend in a light or bright colour. An adult beanie takes about 150 to 200 yards, a child's 110 to 150 and a baby's 60 to 100 (our estimates, the writer gives yours).
  • Needles: a 16 in (40 cm) circular needle in the size for your yarn (5 mm / US 8 for worsted), and a set of double-pointed needles in the same size for the crown, or a long 32 in circular for the magic loop. Baby and toddler hats are too small for a 16 in circular, so work the whole hat on double-pointed needles or magic loop.
  • Also: a stitch marker, a tapestry needle, scissors and a tape measure.

How many stitches to cast on for a hat?

Multiply the hat's width around by your stitches per inch. The hat should be about 10 to 12% smaller than the head so it hugs, so for an average 22 in head you knit a hat of about 19.5 in. At worsted gauge (18 stitches per 4 in, or 4.5 per inch) that's 88 stitches. The writer rounds to a multiple of 8, so the crown decreases come out even and the number also works for k1, p1 and k2, p2 rib. Knit a small swatch in the round first if you can: the cast on calculator and gauge converter work from your own numbers.

How long should a knitted hat be before decreasing?

For a fitted adult beanie, knit until it's about 6 to 6.5 in from the cast-on edge, then decrease; the crown adds the last 2 inches or so. Add 2 to 3 in before decreasing for a fold-up brim and 2 to 3 in for a slouchy hat. Children's hats start the crown at 4.5 to 5.5 in and baby hats at 3.5 to 4.5 in. The writer works it out from the size and your yarn.

How to decrease the crown of a hat

The neatest beginner crown splits the stitches into 8 equal sections and decreases once in each: *knit to 2 stitches before the end of the section, k2tog*, 8 times. Knit one plain round between decrease rounds until about 6 stitches are left in each section, then decrease every round. When 8 stitches are left, cut the yarn with a 6 in tail, thread it through all 8 with a tapestry needle and pull tight. More on spacing decreases evenly.

Knit hat problems and fixes

  • Twisted cast-on: if the first rounds spiral round the needle, there's no fix but starting again. Check before you join. See how to knit in the round.
  • Ladder between double-pointed needles: pull the first two stitches on each needle snug, or shift the stitches along a few each round.
  • Too big: ribbing hides a lot, but a hat more than an inch too big will slip. Measure your gauge in the knit section and use the writer with your number.
  • Dropped a stitch: catch it with a crochet hook: how to fix a dropped stitch.

Gifting or selling hats

A worsted beanie takes about 4 to 6 hours to knit, a chunky one 2 to 3, so a hat is a realistic last-minute Christmas gift: check timings in the handmade gift planner. Selling them? The pricing guide works for knits too. Top it with a pom pom, and if you prefer a hook, here's how to crochet a hat.

Questions crafters ask

How do you knit a hat for beginners?

Cast on in the round on a 16 in circular needle, knit 1.5 to 2 in of rib, knit every round until the hat is about 6 in long, then decrease 8 stitches every other round. Thread the yarn through the last 8 stitches and pull tight.

How many stitches do I cast on for a hat?

For an average adult in worsted yarn, about 88 stitches; in chunky about 64 and in DK about 108. Multiply the hat's width around (about 19.5 in for an adult) by your stitches per inch.

What size circular needle for a hat?

A 16 in (40 cm) circular in the size for your yarn, usually 5 mm (US 8) for worsted. Baby hats are too small for it, so use double-pointed needles or magic loop.

How long should a knitted hat be before decreasing?

About 6 to 6.5 in from the cast-on edge for a fitted adult beanie, 4.5 to 5.5 in for a child and 3.5 to 4.5 in for a baby. Add 2 to 3 in for a fold-up brim or a slouchy hat.

How much yarn do I need to knit a hat?

Roughly 150 to 200 yards of worsted for an adult beanie, 110 to 150 for a child and 60 to 100 for a baby (our estimates). Add more for a folded brim or a pom pom.

Can you knit a hat with straight needles?

Yes: knit it flat as a rectangle with the crown decreases on right-side rows, then sew the side seam with mattress stitch. Knitting in the round avoids the seam.

How do you finish the top of a knitted hat?

When 8 stitches are left, cut the yarn with a 6 in tail, thread it through all the stitches on a tapestry needle, pull tight, push it to the inside and weave it in.
