Bookbinding hole template + stitch guide

Set your signature height and number of sewing stations. Print the punching guide, fold it into your signature and pierce the holes evenly.

Quick answer: a pamphlet stitch uses 3 or 5 holes along the fold. For multi-signature books (coptic, kettle stitch) mark a kettle station about 1 to 1.5 cm from the head and tail and space the rest evenly between them. Paper grain must run parallel to the spine.

Print at "Actual size" (not "Fit to page").

Preview

Three bookbinding stitches to learn first

  1. Pamphlet stitch (one signature): 3 or 5 holes. Sew from the middle hole out, up to the top hole, down past the middle to the bottom hole, back to the middle, and tie off over the long stitch. It takes ten minutes and binds a booklet or junk journal.
  2. Kettle stitch (many signatures): sew along the first signature, then link each new signature to the one below at the head and tail stations with a small loop (the kettle). It's the base of most hand-bound books.
  3. Coptic stitch (lay-flat books): an exposed chain stitch along the spine joins signatures and covers with no glue, so the book opens completely flat. Use waxed linen thread and a curved needle.

Paper grain and folding

Paper folds and turns more easily along its grain. Bend a sheet gently both ways: the direction with less resistance is the grain, and it should run parallel to the spine. Fold signatures with a bone folder, and nest 4 to 6 sheets per signature for most paper. Our signature calculator works out how many sheets you need.

Tools

  • An awl or a thick needle for piercing, and a punching cradle (or an open phone book) to hold the fold.
  • Waxed linen or waxed polyester thread; a blunt bookbinding needle.
  • A bone folder, a metal ruler, a cutting mat and a sharp knife.
  • Printable pages: dot grid, lettering guides or a 2027 calendar.

Questions crafters ask

How many holes for a pamphlet stitch?

3 holes for small booklets, 5 for anything taller than about 15 cm (6 in).

How far from the edge should bookbinding holes be?

Most binders put the end stations 1 to 2 cm (about 1/2 to 3/4 in) from the head and tail, then space the rest evenly.

Which way should the paper grain go in a book?

Parallel to the spine, so pages turn easily and don't warp.
